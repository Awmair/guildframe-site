# Choose a platform from your backers and your product, not from the headline fee.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-vs-indiegogo
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Write down where your existing customers or followers already back projects.
- [ ] Check the current rules for your category on both platforms, including prototype rules for hardware.
- [ ] Compare fees on your real reward prices, including payment processing.
- [ ] Decide how you will take orders after the campaign closes, such as late pledges or your own shop.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: a creator's followers mostly found the product through a Kickstarter newsletter. Even if the fees look similar, launching where those followers already have accounts removes one step on launch day.

## Check your work

You can explain your choice in one sentence that starts with your backers, not with the platform.
