# Answer the four platform questions and write down which platform fits your product and why.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-vs-indiegogo
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Film your prototype working, or note the date it will be ready, because Kickstarter will not accept renders for hardware.
- [ ] Write down how much of your stock is ready or close to ready to ship.
- [ ] Estimate your average pledge and check whether most pledges will pass $125.
- [ ] Put your factory deposit date beside the Kickstarter payout date, 14 days after the deadline plus your bank's time.
- [ ] Ask your fulfillment partner which pledge manager they can import orders from.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you make a $160 desk lamp with a working prototype and no stock yet. You need a goal to pay for the first run, most pledges will pass $125, and your factory deposit is due a month after launch. That points to Kickstarter with Pledge Over Time switched on, and a deposit date moved to after the payout lands.

## Check your work

You can explain your platform choice to a friend in two sentences, naming the rule or tool that decided it.
