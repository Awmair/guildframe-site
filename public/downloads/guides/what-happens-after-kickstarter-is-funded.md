- [ ] Reconcile collected pledges and unresolved payments.

- [ ] Set the next backer update and support owner.

## Planning example

A funding total is not the same as the cash available for production. Prepare a reconciliation that separates expected funds, fees, failed payments and costs already incurred. Keep late orders visible in the quantity plan so they do not silently consume stock intended for backers.

## Check your work

Every outstanding promise should have an owner, next step and date for the next update.
