# Explain what someone can play with the reward they choose.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/card-game-kickstarter-launch-guide
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] State player count and the decks needed to start.
- [ ] Distinguish fixed contents from random assortments.
- [ ] Show card text at a readable size.

## Planning example

If a starter box supports two players while a booster cannot be played alone, say so next to the reward. For collectible products, distinguish the cards shown for illustration from any guaranteed contents. A buyer should not need to infer the format from a dramatic spread of cards.

## Check your work

Ask a new player to pick the smallest reward that lets their group play.
