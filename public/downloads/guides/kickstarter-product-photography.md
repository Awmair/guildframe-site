# Write a shot list from your page outline and brief your photographer with it.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-product-photography
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] List every page section and the image it needs.
- [ ] Add one photo or GIF for each feature you claim.
- [ ] Mark which units are working prototypes and which are models.
- [ ] Choose backgrounds and props that match your page design.
- [ ] Check each shot against the list before you pack up the shoot.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your page outline has eight sections and claims five features. That gives you eight section images and five feature proofs, two of them GIFs because they involve movement. Add a scale shot, a box layout and two lifestyle shots, and you have about eighteen shots for one shoot day.

## Check your work

Every section of your page outline has a named shot, and every feature claim has its proof.
