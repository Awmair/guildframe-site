# Choose a platform from your own backers, not from the headline fee.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-vs-gamefound
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] List where your current followers and subscribers already back projects.
- [ ] Model 5% plus processing on your real reward prices for both platforms.
- [ ] Pick an ad route: Adfound, Jellop or your own Meta ads.
- [ ] Ask your fulfilment partner which pledge manager exports they can import.
- [ ] Decide whether you will sell add ons and late pledges after funding.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you ask your 600 email subscribers where they usually back games, and most of them name Gamefound. Your party card game could reach a wider crowd on Kickstarter, but your known backers point the other way. Record the answers and the reason for your pick, so the decision rests on evidence you can show.

## Check your work

Answer every question in the guide's checklist with a fact from your own list, prices or fulfilment plan, not a guess.
