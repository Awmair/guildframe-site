# Build your two budget Kickstarter cost sheet this week.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-campaign-cost
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Make one column for costs you pay before launch and one for costs paid from pledges.
- [ ] Price samples, photos and video, page design, ads and tools in the first column, even if some are zero.
- [ ] In the second column, list manufacturing, shipping by region, packaging, pledge manager fees, duties and taxes.
- [ ] Add Kickstarter fees at 5% plus 3% and $0.20 a pledge, and a reserve of 20 to 30 percent.
- [ ] Check that you could make and ship every reward at exactly 100% of the goal.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: 500 backers pledge $60 each on a US project, so the campaign raises $30,000. Fees take about $2,500 and a 20% reserve keeps back $6,000. That leaves $21,500 to make, pack and ship every reward, so if your quotes come to more, the goal rises before launch.

## Check your work

Every line in both columns carries a number or a dated question, and the goal covers the second column in full.
