# Turn every agency proposal into one total you can compare.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-agency-cost
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Send each agency the same scope list of pages, emails, graphics, ads and live campaign work.
- [ ] Ask for the total cost at a low, middle and high pledge figure.
- [ ] Add the same ad budget and platform fees to every proposal.
- [ ] Ask in writing how commission pledges are attributed.
- [ ] Confirm who owns the files, ad accounts and email list at the end.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: one agency quotes a fixed fee and another asks for a share of pledges. Estimate your pledge total at three levels and multiply the commission out at each one. If the commission passes the fixed fee at your middle figure, you can see where the cheaper looking offer stops being cheaper.

## Check your work

Each proposal should show its scope, its total at three pledge levels and the costs it leaves to you, side by side on one page.
