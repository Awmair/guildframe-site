# Answer size, fit and contents for every reward before you write the page.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/terrain-and-dice-kickstarter-launch-guide
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Measure every product and put the confirmed dimensions beside its image.
- [ ] Photograph each set as separate parts and as two or three builds.
- [ ] Label every image as a render, test print, painted sample or production piece.
- [ ] Decide which rewards are physical, which are STL files and which add ons each can take.
- [ ] Get the packed box size for every reward and likely bundle.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: a terrain set packs into a 16 × 12 × 6 inch box. At UPS daily rates that is 1,152 cubic inches divided by 139, which rounds up to 9 pounds of dimensional weight, even if the box weighs 3 pounds. Ask your fulfilment partner to quote that packed box, then price shipping from their figure.

## Check your work

Cover the images and read only the reward text: it should still say what arrives, how big it is and what it builds.
