# Make essential reward facts readable without zooming.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-campaign-graphics-mobile-readability
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Keep product specifications in page text.
- [ ] Break wide comparison sheets into narrower sections.
- [ ] Test image text on a small phone.

## Planning example

A six-column reward matrix may look fine on a design monitor but become illegible when scaled to a phone. Split it by edition or compare only the differences. Use short labels and put the complete contents in nearby text so the image is not the only usable version.

## Check your work

Check at actual display width and ordinary brightness, without pinching to enlarge.
