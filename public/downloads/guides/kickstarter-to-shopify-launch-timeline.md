# Launch retail when inventory and customer promises are ready.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-to-shopify-launch-timeline
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Map backer obligations before planning stock availability.
- [ ] Prepare products and policies while fulfilment work continues.
- [ ] Run complete test orders before announcing the store.

## Planning example

The store can be built before every parcel ships, but public availability should reflect the stock you can actually sell. If a product is a preorder, show its expected timing separately from in-stock products and explain what happens to mixed orders.

## Check your work

Confirm the support, inventory and fulfilment owners all agree the store is ready.
