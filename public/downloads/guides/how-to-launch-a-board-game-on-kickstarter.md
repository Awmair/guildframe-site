# Place your game on the launch timeline and find the first stage that isn't done.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/how-to-launch-a-board-game-on-kickstarter
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Write today's date and your hoped for launch date at the top of one page.
- [ ] Mark each stage as done, started or not started: the game, the numbers, prelaunch, the page and review.
- [ ] Build the budget before the goal, with fees, shipping and a reserve of 20 to 30 percent.
- [ ] Put your prelaunch page and email signup live as soon as you can show the game.
- [ ] Count back from launch, allowing up to three business days for review and three more for changes.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your game is playtested and the cover art is done, but you have no manufacturer quote and no prelaunch page. On the example timeline, the numbers sit four to six months before launch and the prelaunch page three to four months before. So a launch eight weeks from now is too early, and this week's job is asking for quotes and opening the prelaunch page.

## Check your work

Every stage before your launch date should be marked done or have a date, and the goal should come from the budget rather than the reverse.
