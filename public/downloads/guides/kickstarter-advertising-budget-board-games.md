# Set a spending limit that your reward economics can support.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-advertising-budget-board-games
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Calculate contribution after production and delivery obligations.
- [ ] Separate a learning budget from a scaling budget.
- [ ] Set a review date and stop condition before spending.

## Planning example

If a hypothetical reward leaves $12 after non-ad costs and your reserve, a $10 acquisition cost leaves only $2. A higher-priced bundle may produce more revenue while leaving less contribution. Compare the actual reward mix before increasing a budget because reported return on ad spend looks strong.

## Check your work

The budget sheet should show service fees, media spend, attribution limits and the person who can approve an increase.
