# Outline your page around the four questions a backer asks.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/how-to-design-a-kickstarter-page
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Write one line each for what the game is, how it plays, what backers get and why they can trust you.
- [ ] Draft the top: project image, title, a subtitle for a stranger and the table facts.
- [ ] Pick one turn to show, with what a player sees, chooses and what happens next.
- [ ] List every reward with labelled contents, counts and prices in one place.
- [ ] Set two fonts, colours taken from the box art and a minimum text size for phones.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: a tester reads your draft on a phone and reaches the stretch goals before learning how a turn works. They also find the deluxe price in one section and its contents in another. You move the sample turn above the stretch goals and put each reward's price beside its contents, then test again with someone new.

## Check your work

Hand a phone to someone who hasn't played the game: they should explain a turn and pick a reward without asking you anything.
