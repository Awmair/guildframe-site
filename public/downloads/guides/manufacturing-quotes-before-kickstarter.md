# Send one complete quote request to at least three factories.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/manufacturing-quotes-before-kickstarter
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Put drawings, bill of materials, materials and packaging into one pack.
- [ ] Ask for prices at your goal, expected and strong quantities.
- [ ] Ask for tooling, samples and packaging on separate lines.
- [ ] Ask where the price ends, such as EXW or FOB, and how long it is valid.
- [ ] Turn the best quote into landed cost and a draft funding goal.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: one factory quotes $17.90 a unit with a 2,000 unit MOQ, and another quotes $18.20 with a 1,000 unit MOQ. With the first, your goal would have to fund 2,000 units, nearly double the stock. The second quote is the cheaper campaign even though its unit price is higher.

## Check your work

Your funding goal covers the full minimum order, tooling, samples, freight, a reserve and fees.
