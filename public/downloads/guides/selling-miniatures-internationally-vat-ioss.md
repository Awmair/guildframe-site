Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Record dispatch country, destination and goods value.
- [ ] Confirm classifications, origin and packed dimensions.
- [ ] Test the agreed duty and tax treatment at checkout.

## Planning example

A parcel containing different products can need more than one customs classification. Record actual contents rather than a broad label such as gaming accessories. Ask the carrier how it transmits the required data and how any IOSS arrangement is applied to eligible orders.

## Check your work

Keep a dated record of the advice and carrier setup. Recheck rules before opening a new market.
