# Find the first gap between your gadget and a launch you can deliver.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/tech-gadget-kickstarter-launch-guide
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Film the working prototype doing its main job in one continuous take.
- [ ] Ask a factory to quote three quantities, with tooling and samples on separate lines.
- [ ] Get a written FCC and CE test plan and price from a test lab.
- [ ] Ask your cell maker for the UN 38.3 test summary and your fulfillment partner for a written yes on batteries.
- [ ] Draft a delivery timeline with DVT, certification, PVT, freight and a buffer.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your Bluetooth speaker works on the bench, but no factory has quoted it and you have not spoken to a test lab. The readiness check stops at the second question, so this week is for quote requests, not page design. Once three quotes and a lab plan are back, you can set a goal and a delivery month you can defend.

## Check your work

Every question in the readiness check has a yes, or a dated plan to get one.
