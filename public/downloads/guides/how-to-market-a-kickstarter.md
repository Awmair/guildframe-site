# Write your player sentence and set a weekly marketing habit you can keep.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/how-to-market-a-kickstarter
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Describe your player in one sentence and list three games they already own.
- [ ] Set up your prelaunch page, a reason to follow and an email signup.
- [ ] Pick two or three channels that suit your player and read each community's rules.
- [ ] Each week, post one thing that shows the game and note where new followers came from.
- [ ] Hold paid ads until the landing page turns visitors into followers.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your player sentence is people who love quick card games they can teach in five minutes. The games on their shelves point you to two card game subreddits and a local game night, so those become your channels. Each week you post one short clip of a turn, invite viewers to the prelaunch page and note which channel each new follower came from.

## Check your work

Add up what friends, family, followers and your email list could pledge on day one, then compare it with 30% of your goal.
