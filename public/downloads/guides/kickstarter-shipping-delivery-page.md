# Give backers a clear account of when and how delivery charges are collected.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-shipping-delivery-page
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Separate manufacturing, freight, fulfilment and final delivery.
- [ ] Label regional figures as estimates where appropriate.
- [ ] State how taxes and import charges are handled.

## Planning example

A reward table that says shipping is separate needs a nearby explanation of when it will be charged. Show the planned region list and the assumptions behind estimates, such as one core game per parcel. A bundle can change the parcel size enough to need its own estimate.

## Check your work

Read the page as a buyer in your most expensive supported region and identify every payment they may face.
