## Planning example

For a cooperative card game, one week might produce a filmed sample turn, a landing-page test and a welcome email. If visitors sign up but cannot explain what the box contains, spend the next week fixing the offer before expanding traffic. A schedule should change when the evidence changes.

## Check your work

Every scheduled task should produce something reviewable: a file, a tested flow, a quote or a decision.
