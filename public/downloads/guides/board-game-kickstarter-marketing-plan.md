Author: Umair at Guildframe

- [ ] List the open product, reward and shipping decisions.

- [ ] Review audience evidence before committing the launch budget.

## Planning example

For a cooperative card game, one week might produce a filmed sample turn, a landing page test and a welcome email. If visitors sign up but cannot say what the box contains, spend the next week fixing the offer before buying more traffic. A schedule should change when the evidence changes.

## Check your work

Every scheduled task should produce something you can review, such as a file, a tested flow, a quote or a decision.
