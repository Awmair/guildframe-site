## Planning example

Suppose your factory requires 1,000 copies but the campaign model includes only 500 pledged games. Record the full purchase commitment and the cash needed for unsold copies. If you expect retail sales to cover that gap later, name the financing available before those sales happen.

## Check your work

At the exact funding target, the budget must cover the minimum purchase commitment and all promised rewards.
