# Build a store without losing the campaign's delivery obligations.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/move-from-kickstarter-to-shopify
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Map rewards to retail products and variants.
- [ ] Keep backer orders separate from new retail orders.
- [ ] Plan redirects and content reuse before launch.

## Planning example

A campaign bundle may contain a limited extra that will not be sold at retail. Create a product map before importing descriptions. Mark which images need new captions and which availability claims no longer apply. Importing all campaign language unchanged can create promises the store cannot fulfil.

## Check your work

Place a test order for a new customer and check that it cannot consume reserved backer inventory.
