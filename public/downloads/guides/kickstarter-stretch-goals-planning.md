# Approve each extra before turning it into a public promise.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-stretch-goals-planning
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Price the added unit cost at realistic quantities.
- [ ] Check tooling, weight and packing changes.
- [ ] State exactly which rewards receive the extra.

## Planning example

A thicker token may look like a small upgrade but change the insert, box height and packed weight. Ask the manufacturer and fulfilment partner to price the whole change. A goal is not ready simply because the print cost fits inside the additional funding.

## Check your work

Keep an approval record for cost, timing, eligible rewards and any effect on existing backers.
