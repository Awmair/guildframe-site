# Build a two column budget: costs before launch and costs paid from pledges.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-product-campaign-cost
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] List every cost you pay before launch: prototypes, photos or renders, video, the campaign page and prelaunch marketing.
- [ ] List every cost paid from pledges: manufacturing, packaging, shipping, fulfilment and platform fees.
- [ ] Apply Kickstarter's current fee rates to your expected total, using your own country's schedule.
- [ ] Mark which before launch costs you can pay from savings and which need a quote.
- [ ] Add a buffer line for surprises, such as a second prototype or a certification test.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: a product needs two prototype rounds, a photo shoot and a campaign page before launch. Writing those down before setting the goal shows the creator needs savings for the first column, because none of it can come from pledges.

## Check your work

Every line has a number or a quote request beside it, and the before launch column is fully covered by money you already have.
