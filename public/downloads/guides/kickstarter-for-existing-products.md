# Decide whether your new version counts as new, then plan how past buyers hear about it.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-for-existing-products
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] List every change from version one in your customers' words, and cut anything that is only a new color.
- [ ] Photograph version one and the version two prototype side by side.
- [ ] Count your version one stock and choose to sell it down, bundle it as an add on or hold it.
- [ ] Draft a Secret Reward for past buyers priced just below the early bird.
- [ ] Write the first update to your old backers and the first email to customers who agreed to hear from you.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: version one sold at $45 and version two adds a new material and a better clasp, retailing at $69. You set an early bird at $49, a past buyer Secret Reward at $47 and a version one add on at $25. Your old backers get the prelaunch link three weeks before launch.

## Check your work

A stranger can say what changed from one photo, and your past buyer offer, add on and announcement dates are all written down.
