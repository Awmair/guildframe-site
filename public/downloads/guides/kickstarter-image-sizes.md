# Build your export list and test one section before you make the rest.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-image-sizes
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Copy the cheat sheet rows for your platform into an export list.
- [ ] Plan the crops your artwork needs, such as 16:9 for Kickstarter or a square and a 21:6 strip for Gamefound.
- [ ] Export one story graphic and one reward image at the sizes in the table.
- [ ] Upload both to the draft and check the preview on a phone.
- [ ] Decide on the main video and the vertical clip before anyone films.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you export a story graphic at 1400 pixels wide and a reward image at 1200 × 800 for Kickstarter. In the phone preview the reward image reads well, but small text in the story graphic blurs after compression. You fix the text size now, while one file needs changing instead of forty.

## Check your work

Every file on the export list should meet or beat the published minimum, and the test section should read on a phone without zooming.
