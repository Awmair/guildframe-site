Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Decide what each report is meant to answer
- [ ] Create named Kickstarter links before you publish the campaign
- [ ] Use one naming convention for your traffic links

## Planning example

Give a rules-preview email and a launch email separate placement names. Send test messages and copy the destination reached after clicking each one. Keep the actual Kickstarter-generated referral URL intact. This lets you trace an unexpected result back to a specific placement instead of guessing from a campaign total.

## Check your work

Every live link should have a destination, owner, test date and named report.
