# Put a dated tariff line, confirmed by your broker, into each US reward price.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/board-game-tariffs-shipping-us
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] List each reward's contents with the country where each item is made.
- [ ] Ask your customs broker for the HTS code and full duty rate on each item.
- [ ] Add the duty to your landed cost before reward prices are final.
- [ ] Decide whether US duty sits in the reward price, the shipping charge or a Tariff Manager surcharge.
- [ ] Write a dated tariff note for the shipping section of your page.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: a game costs $12 a copy at a factory in China, and duty is charged on that $12. The 12.5% Section 301 rate alone adds $1.50 a copy. Your broker may find other duties on top, so the reward price uses the broker's full figure rather than this one rate.

## Check your work

Your page, pledge manager and backer updates should all give US backers the same tariff answer, with the date it was checked.
