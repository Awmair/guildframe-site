# Find your category's real numbers and test your funding goal against them this week.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-statistics-by-category
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Write down your category and its success rate from the table, with the period it covers.
- [ ] Set your planned goal beside the funding brackets and note what share of successful projects raised more.
- [ ] Count your likely day one backers from your email list and prelaunch followers.
- [ ] Multiply that count by your expected average pledge and compare it with the goal.
- [ ] Mark every statistic you plan to quote in your page or pitch with its source and date.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you plan a $24,000 goal for a Design project with an expected $60 average pledge. That needs 400 backers, and it is more than about 80% of successful projects raised as of January 2025. You have 150 people on your list, so you either grow the audience before launch or lower the goal to what your costs truly need.

## Check your work

You can say in one sentence how your goal compares with your category and where the backers to reach it will come from.
