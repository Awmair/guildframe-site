# Plan your version two campaign around what changed and who already owns version one.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-version-two
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] List what is new in version two and why each change matters to a buyer.
- [ ] Gather proof from version one: reviews, sales, returns and common questions.
- [ ] Decide what existing owners get, such as an upgrade reward or early access.
- [ ] Check the platform's rules on existing products and relaunches before writing the page.
- [ ] Write the one sentence that tells a version one owner why to back again.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: a creator's first product sold well but owners asked for a stronger clip. Leading the page with that change, and showing the old and new clip side by side, speaks to past buyers and new visitors at once.

## Check your work

A version one owner can read the top of the page and say what is new and why it is worth backing.
