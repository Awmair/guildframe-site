# Answer the six readiness questions and turn each no into a dated task.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/is-my-game-ready-for-kickstarter
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Run a blind test where a stranger plays from the written rules while you say nothing.
- [ ] Photograph a full set of components and label each image as prototype, render or final.
- [ ] Ask a manufacturer for a written quote on your smallest realistic print run.
- [ ] Get a rough shipping cost for one copy to your main countries.
- [ ] Name the people who will back you on day one and how you will reach them.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you answer yes on the rules, the photos and the cover art, but you have no print quote and no shipping figure. So the next month goes on quotes, plus the 20 to 30 percent contingency Kickstarter suggests as a starting point. Meanwhile your prelaunch page can go live, so it gathers followers while the numbers come in.

## Check your work

Every no should have an owner and a date, and your launch date should come after the last of them.
