# Keep platform review separate from creative approval.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-review-launch-timeline
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Identify the last facts needed for a complete submission.
- [ ] Allow time for requested changes and resubmission.
- [ ] Confirm approval before promising a public launch time.

## Planning example

A finished hero graphic does not settle identity checks, reward setup or the production plan. Work backwards from the intended date and give each dependency its own owner. Keep promotional bookings flexible until the project is ready to publish.

## Check your work

The launch calendar should include a fallback date and the people who need to know if the date changes.
