# Outline your product page section by section before any design starts.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/product-kickstarter-page
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Write the top: project image, title and a subtitle a stranger understands.
- [ ] List the three problems the product solves and the proof for each, such as a test or a photo.
- [ ] Plan the reward section so every option can be compared side by side.
- [ ] Add the facts backers check before pledging: specifications, materials, delivery dates and shipping regions.
- [ ] Write the risks and challenges section in plain words.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: a page opens with lifestyle photos but only explains what the product does halfway down. Moving one clear photo of the product in use and a short line of what it does to the top answers the first question a visitor has.

## Check your work

Show the outline to a stranger on a phone. If they can say what the product is and what they would pledge for within a minute, the outline works.
