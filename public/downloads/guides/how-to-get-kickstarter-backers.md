# List your first hundred people, ring by ring, and give each one a place to follow.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/how-to-get-kickstarter-backers
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Write down everyone who has played your game, with how to reach them.
- [ ] Add the local game stores and game nights where you can run a demo.
- [ ] Join three communities where your players talk, and read their self promotion rules.
- [ ] Have your prelaunch page, email signup and one thing to show ready before you ask.
- [ ] Ask each person to follow, join the list and name one friend who would enjoy the game.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: with a $50 average pledge, 100 backers cover a $5,000 goal. You count 25 playtesters, 15 friends and family and 30 people from two store demos. That leaves 30 people to find through communities, reviewers or a small ad test, so you know where the next month of effort goes.

## Check your work

Every name on the list should have a ring, the date you asked and whether they followed the page or joined the email list.
