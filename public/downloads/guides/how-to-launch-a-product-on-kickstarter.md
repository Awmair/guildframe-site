# Find the first launch stage your product hasn't finished and give it a date.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/how-to-launch-a-product-on-kickstarter
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Film your working prototype doing every main feature, with real hands in a real setting.
- [ ] Ask two or three factories for written quotes with unit price, MOQ, tooling and lead time.
- [ ] Build a budget from the minimum order, fees of about 8% plus $0.20 a pledge, fulfillment and a 20 to 30 percent reserve.
- [ ] Get shipping quotes for your top five backer countries using the packed weight.
- [ ] Put a signup page live with real photos and count signups each week.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your prototype works and is filmed, but you only have one factory quote and no signup page. The MOQ in that quote is 500 units at $28 landed, so your goal has to cover $14,000 of production before fees and a reserve. This week's jobs are a second quote and a live signup page.

## Check your work

Every stage before your launch date is marked done or has a date, and the goal comes from the minimum order rather than a round number.
