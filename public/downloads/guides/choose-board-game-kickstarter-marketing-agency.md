- [ ] Send the same deliverables list to each agency.
- [ ] Separate service fees, media spend and any revenue share.
- [ ] Ask who owns the ad account, contacts and editable files.

## Planning example

Suppose one quote includes a landing page and six emails while another covers only campaign graphics. Put the missing work beside each total before comparing prices. Ask each agency to identify the person who checks reward facts and approves publication.

## Check your work

A proposal is ready to compare when the work, dates, approval rules and handover files are named.
