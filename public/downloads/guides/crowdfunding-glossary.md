# Learn the terms for the stage you are at and turn each one into a decision for your project.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/crowdfunding-glossary
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Pick the glossary group that matches where your project is today.
- [ ] Read each term in that group and mark the ones that need a decision from you.
- [ ] Write your answer beside each marked term, such as your goal, your tiers or your payout date.
- [ ] Ask suppliers about MOQ, unit cost, lead time and landed cost in writing.
- [ ] Open the linked guide for any term you still cannot answer in one sentence.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you are building the page, so you work through the page and rewards group. You decide on three reward tiers, two add ons and one stretch goal, and you note that a hardware project needs a working prototype on film. Then you move to the money group and work out the fees on your main tier.
