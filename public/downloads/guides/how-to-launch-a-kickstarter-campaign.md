# Find the first stage of your Kickstarter launch that isn't done and give it a date this week.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/how-to-launch-a-kickstarter-campaign
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Confirm you live in a supported country and can verify ID, a local bank account and a card.
- [ ] Run your idea through the fit questions: something new, a clear end point and rewards you made.
- [ ] Build the budget before the goal, with production, shipping, fees, ads and a reserve.
- [ ] Open an email signup page as soon as you can show a working prototype or sample.
- [ ] Count back from launch: review four to six weeks out, then the prelaunch page after approval.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you have a working prototype of a $49 product and one factory quote, but no shipping quote and no signup page. Shipping is stage seven and the signup page belongs to stage two, so a launch six weeks from now is too early. This week's job is a packed weight, two more quotes and a live signup page.

## Check your work

Every one of the ten stages is marked done or has a date, and your goal comes from the budget rather than the other way round.
