# Price your book campaign from real print and postage numbers this week.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/how-to-launch-a-book-on-kickstarter
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Ask two printers for quotes at two or three run sizes, offset and print on demand.
- [ ] Order one proof copy and check color, paper and layout.
- [ ] Weigh one packed copy of each format, mailer included.
- [ ] List one ISBN for each format you will sell.
- [ ] Name your first 25 likely backers and where you will reach them.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your paperback costs $6 a copy on print on demand and weighs just under a pound packed. You plan a $25 signed paperback tier, so after fees, print and Media Mail postage you can see what each pledge leaves for editing and a reserve. If 25 backers at that tier would not cover the goal, you add an ebook tier and a limited hardcover before you launch.

## Check your work

Every reward tier should show its print cost, postage and fees, and the goal should still cover them at your expected backer count.
