## Planning example

Use a welcome email to explain one appealing decision in the game and link to the rules or a sample turn. A reply asking whether the reward is a physical deck or a PDF exposes a message problem you can fix before launch. Track replies alongside clicks; open rates alone do not show purchase intent.

## Check your work

Subscribe with a fresh address, confirm the right message arrives and test the unsubscribe route.
