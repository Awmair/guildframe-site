- [ ] Prepare a clear game summary and representative assets.
- [ ] Identify reward and production decisions still open.
- [ ] Agree a date for freezing the first full layout.

## Planning example

You can explore an opening design while later sections remain incomplete. Tell the designer which facts may change, especially reward names, box contents and delivery estimates. A late change to the reward structure can affect many graphics, so schedule that decision before final export.

## Check your work

The schedule should allow the creator to review facts and the designer to check the assembled page.
