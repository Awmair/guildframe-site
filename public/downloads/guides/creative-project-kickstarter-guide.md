# Shape your idea into one Kickstarter project with a floor goal and a community ready to back it.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/creative-project-kickstarter-guide
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Write the one thing you will make and the date it is finished.
- [ ] Check that any charity share is 10% or less and that no reward pays money back.
- [ ] List only the costs needed to finish and deliver, plus fees and a reserve.
- [ ] Write down 30 people likely to back on day one and how you will reach them.
- [ ] Pick a digital entry tier, one physical tier and one limited experience.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your band wants to record an EP and press vinyl. The floor that finishes the record is $3,000, while a music video would push it to $6,000. You set the goal at $3,000, make the video a stretch goal, and check that 30 named fans at an average $40 pledge plus a $5 download tier could get you most of the way.

## Check your work

You can say in one sentence what backers get and when, and your first 30 backers could cover at least a third of the goal.
