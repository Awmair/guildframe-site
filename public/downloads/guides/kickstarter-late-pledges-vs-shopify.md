## Planning example

If production quantities are still open, a late-pledge route may fit the campaign workflow. Once fulfilment and retail inventory are separated, a permanent store may be easier to operate. Avoid taking the same customer's payment twice when moving between pledge collection and shipping collection.

## Check your work

Write down which system owns each order, address, payment and customer-support record.
