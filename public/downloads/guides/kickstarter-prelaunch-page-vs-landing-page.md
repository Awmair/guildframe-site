# Choose where each visitor should go before launch.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-prelaunch-page-vs-landing-page
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Give cold traffic one clear explanation and signup action.
- [ ] Invite interested subscribers to follow the platform page.
- [ ] Measure signups and platform follows separately.

## Planning example

A new visitor from a gameplay clip may need a playable example before sharing an email. Someone already reading your newsletter may only need the Kickstarter follow link. Keep those paths distinct so you can diagnose a weak ad without blaming the platform page.

## Check your work

Follow both paths on a phone and check what the visitor sees after completing the action.
