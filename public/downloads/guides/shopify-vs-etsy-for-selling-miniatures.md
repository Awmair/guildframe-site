Author: Umair at Guildframe

- [ ] Separate marketplace discovery from traffic you bring yourself.
- [ ] Model the fees on a representative order.
- [ ] Check licence terms and marketplace eligibility.

## Planning example

For a physical terrain kit, include packaging, payment processing and any applicable advertising charges in the comparison. For digital files, check the platform's rules for the actual product and your rights to sell it. A cheaper headline fee does not show the total cost of acquiring an order.

## Check your work

Use the same product price, delivery region and order assumptions for both channels.
