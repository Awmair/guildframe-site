# Put everything your campaign designer needs into one brief before the first call.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-campaign-brief
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Write one sentence on what the product is and who it is for.
- [ ] Gather your best photos, renders and any video, each labelled as prototype, render or final.
- [ ] List every reward with its contents, price and estimated delivery month.
- [ ] Add your launch window, your funding goal and the platform you plan to use.
- [ ] Note what you already have, such as a logo, an email list or product copy, and what is still missing.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: a creator sends a strap prototype photo, a rough price and a target month. The designer can start, but only after a second round of questions about rewards, shipping regions and which photos are final. Sending those answers in the first brief saves about a week.

## Check your work

Give the brief to someone who has never seen the product. If they can say what it is, who it is for and what a backer receives, it is ready to send.
