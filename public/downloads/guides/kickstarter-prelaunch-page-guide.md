# Give a follower enough information to recognize the project at launch.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-prelaunch-page-guide
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Show the product and its central play experience.
- [ ] Check the public page separately from the private preview.
- [ ] Use the same game name and visual identity in email.

## Planning example

A title, dramatic illustration and vague promise can attract curiosity without explaining the game. Add a short line about player decisions and the intended audience. When the launch email arrives, the person should recognize the same product rather than wonder which signup it came from.

## Check your work

Ask an unfamiliar reader to describe the product after seeing only the public prelaunch page.
