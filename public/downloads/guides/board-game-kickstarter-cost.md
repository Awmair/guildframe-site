# Build your two column launch budget this week.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/board-game-kickstarter-cost
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Make one column for costs you pay before launch and one for costs paid from pledges.
- [ ] Put a number beside every before launch line: prototypes, art, reviewer copies, the page and marketing.
- [ ] Mark any line you can't price yet as a question, and ask for the biggest quotes first.
- [ ] In the second column, list manufacturing, shipping, customs, packaging, handling and fees.
- [ ] Add a reserve, starting from the 20 to 30 percent Kickstarter suggests.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: 750 backers pledge $40 each on a US project, so the campaign raises $30,000. Fees take about $2,550 and a 20% reserve keeps back $6,000. That leaves about $21,450 to make, ship and deliver every game, so if your print and freight quotes come to more, the goal has to rise before you launch.

## Check your work

Every line in both columns should carry a number or a named question, and the second column should still balance at your exact funding goal.
