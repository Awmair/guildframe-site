# Check your page against Kickstarter's prototype rules before you submit it for review.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-prototype-rules
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Film the real prototype doing its core job and keep the clip on the page.
- [ ] Replace every photorealistic render with a photo or a clearly illustrated drawing.
- [ ] Label each prototype image with its stage.
- [ ] Write the production section: maker, stage, steps left and your track record.
- [ ] Answer the generative AI questions and say which parts, if any, used AI.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your page opens with a lifelike render of a desk lamp because the final shell is not molded yet. Swap it for a photo of the working prototype with a stage label, and move a line drawing of the final shape further down. The page now shows proof first and the future design second.

## Check your work

No image on the page could be mistaken for a finished product that does not exist yet.
