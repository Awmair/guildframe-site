# Approve the margin on each reward.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/board-game-kickstarter-reward-pricing
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Compare the core and deluxe reward side by side.
- [ ] Check packed dimensions as well as product weight.
- [ ] Test bundles with the actual quantities and discounts.
- [ ] Apply your country's current Kickstarter fee rates to every tier.

## Planning example

Compare a core box with a deluxe box that adds a playmat. Ask for the packed size of both. If the mat moves the parcel into a higher bracket, include that difference before choosing the upgrade price. Then check that a discounted bundle still leaves a margin after the extra handling.

## Check your work

Reconcile the pricing sheet with the final reward contents and the fulfilment quotes.
