## Planning example

A page copied from a campaign stage may describe delivery collection that happens later. Review the full order route, including the summary and confirmation. Use Gamefound's testing facilities for supported scenarios and record the stage used in each test.

## Check your work

Keep a test order matrix covering a core reward, a bundle, an add-on and representative delivery regions.
