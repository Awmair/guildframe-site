# Check your three readiness signals, then work back from a real date.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/when-to-launch-a-kickstarter
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Estimate how much of your goal your email list could raise in the first 24 hours.
- [ ] Show the preview to someone who hasn't played and ask them to explain the game back.
- [ ] Confirm a manufacturer quote, a shipping estimate and a goal that covers fees and a reserve.
- [ ] Write a real date beside each step of the backward plan, starting from the slowest job.
- [ ] Check that month for conventions you're attending and big launches in your niche.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you pick a Tuesday launch eight weeks away. Working back, reviewer copies need up to a month before a post appears, and review can take up to three business days plus three more for changes. If the reviewer step already lands in the past, the date is too early, and moving it now costs less than launching thin.

## Check your work

Every step in the backward plan should fit before launch day with a week to spare, and all three signals should be true.
