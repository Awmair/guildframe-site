# Run the five failure checks on your campaign a few weeks before launch.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/why-kickstarter-campaigns-fail
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Count the people already following the project and on your email list.
- [ ] Show the page to five people who have never seen the game and ask them to explain it back.
- [ ] Rebuild the goal from your smallest print run, freight, fulfillment, fees and a buffer.
- [ ] Look at every photo, date and shipping note as a stranger deciding whether to trust you.
- [ ] Write down why you chose the launch date, and move it if your readiness disagrees.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: three of your five testers can't say how a turn works, and your followers are mostly friends and family. Those are two of the causes this guide names, and both show before launch. Moving the date by a month with a short, honest update costs far less than a slow start, because the goal and deadline lock once you launch.

## Check your work

Answer each of the five questions with someone who will tell you the truth, and fix every shaky answer before you confirm the date.
