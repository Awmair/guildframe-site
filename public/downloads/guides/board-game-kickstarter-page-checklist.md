## Planning example

Ask a tester to find the player count, explain a turn and choose a reward within a few minutes. Record where they hesitate without prompting them. If several testers miss shipping information, move or rewrite that information instead of adding another decorative section.

## Check your work

Repeat the task after revisions with someone who has not seen the earlier version.
