# Turn your factory quote into options and rewards that clear every minimum.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/watch-fashion-accessories-kickstarter-guide
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Get the MOQ per style and per color in writing.
- [ ] Approve fit samples and photo samples in every color you plan to show.
- [ ] Draft a size curve from your sales, waitlist or a follower poll.
- [ ] List every material and sustainability claim with the document that proves it.
- [ ] Write a returns and exchanges policy for size and for defects.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your factory's MOQ is 300 units per color, and a poll suggests only 15% of followers want the third color. At 1,000 backers that color would sell about 150 units, half its minimum. So you launch with two colors and offer the third as a stretch goal that unlocks only if its orders clear 300.

## Check your work

Every color and size you offer should clear its minimum at your expected backer count, and every claim on the page should have a document on file.
