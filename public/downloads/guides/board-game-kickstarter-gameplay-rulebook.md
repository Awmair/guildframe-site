# Make one complete turn understandable before asking for a pledge.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/board-game-kickstarter-gameplay-rulebook
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Show the starting position and the player's options.
- [ ] Explain a choice and its consequence.
- [ ] Link the current rules with a visible version date.

## Planning example

For a set-collection game, show a hand, the cards available to take and the scoring change after a choice. An exploded component image cannot substitute for that explanation. Keep unusual terms beside their definitions so the example does not require reading the entire rulebook first.

## Check your work

A reader should be able to describe why they would choose one action over another.
