## Planning example

A generated product section may look correct while linking an expansion to the wrong edition. Keep a small set of representative products and repeat the same purchase tasks after each change. Preserve a rollback version before publishing theme edits.

## Check your work

A successful preview is not proof of payment, order routing or recipient delivery; test those outcomes separately.
