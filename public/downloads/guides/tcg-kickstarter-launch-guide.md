# Settle collation, print minimums and reward limits with your printer first.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/tcg-kickstarter-launch-guide
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Name the smallest reward that lets two people start playing.
- [ ] Ask your printer in writing how it randomizes boosters and whether it supports rarities.
- [ ] Ask how your card count lays out on its press sheets and what the minimum order is.
- [ ] Put booster boxes in fixed tiers and set a quantity limit on the most sought after tier.
- [ ] Write every pack description from the printer's confirmed specification.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you plan 100 card designs in commons, uncommons and rares. PrintNinja says it randomizes only on orders above 20,000 units and does not offer rarities, so a page printed there could not promise rare cards. A printer with rarity tiers and drop rates, as QP Market Network describes, changes what your booster copy can say.

## Check your work

Every rarity or pack claim on the page should match a line in the printer's written specification, and go no further.
