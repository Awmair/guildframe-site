# Prepare one approved source of product facts.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/what-to-send-kickstarter-campaign-designer
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Separate approved assets from drafts.
- [ ] Write reward names, contents and prices in one sheet.
- [ ] Name the person who approves factual changes.

## Planning example

Use filenames that distinguish prototype, final and reference art. Include a short note explaining which images may be cropped and which show dimensions that must stay legible. A designer can build the page faster when they are not reconciling conflicting reward lists from messages.

## Check your work

Someone outside the project should be able to locate the latest logo, rules, reward sheet and delivery assumptions.
