# Make your five shipping decisions and write each one into your campaign plan.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-shipping-physical-products
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Weigh and measure each reward boxed, including the heaviest likely add on combination.
- [ ] Get written quotes from two fulfillment models for your top backer countries.
- [ ] Decide whether you pay duties upfront or backers pay on delivery, and write it for the page.
- [ ] Choose to charge shipping at pledge or later, and set the estimate range you will show.
- [ ] Check the current US entry rules for parcels and the EU rules for orders of 150 euros or less.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your boxed product weighs 900 grams and most backers are in the US, UK and Germany. A warehouse quote for the US and a direct quote for Europe come in, but neither is firm for next spring. So you charge shipping later, show an estimate range on the page, and say clearly that duties are paid before delivery.

## Check your work

Each reward has a shipping price or an estimate range for every country you serve, and the page says who pays duties.
