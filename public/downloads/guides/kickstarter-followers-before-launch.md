# Turn your funding goal into a follower target you can track every week.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-followers-before-launch
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Divide your funding goal by your expected average pledge to get a backer target.
- [ ] Divide that backer target by 10%, by 20 to 25% and by 32% to get three scenarios.
- [ ] List where your current followers came from, warmest source first.
- [ ] Record your follower and email counts on the same day each week.
- [ ] Two weeks before launch, check whether the low case covers the goal.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: a card game has a $15,000 goal and an expected $50 average pledge, so it needs 300 backers. At 10% that means 3,000 followers, at 20 to 25% it means 1,200 to 1,500, and at 32% about 940. If you have 900 followers two weeks out, moving the date may cost less than a slow first day.

## Check your work

Your plan should fund in the low case from followers and your own email list, with platform traffic and ads left as extra.
