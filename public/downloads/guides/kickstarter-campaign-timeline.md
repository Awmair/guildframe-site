# Turn your launch date into a 12 week plan with one visible result due each week.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-campaign-timeline
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Write your launch date at the top of a page and count back 12 weeks to find week 1.
- [ ] Check the basics first: a working prototype, production quotes, a packed weight and eligibility.
- [ ] Verify your Kickstarter account in week 1 and put the signup page live in week 2.
- [ ] Mark the end of week 6 as the day you send the project to review.
- [ ] Add the campaign end date, 14 days of pledge collection and a delivery month you can hit.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you want to launch on Tuesday, April 13, 2027. Week 1 starts on January 19, the signup page goes live the week of January 26 and the project goes to review by March 1. Approval in early March opens the prelaunch page with about five weeks to collect followers before launch.

## Check your work

Every week from now to launch has a named result, and review is booked at least four weeks before launch day.
