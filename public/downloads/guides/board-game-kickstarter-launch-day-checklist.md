# Put a name beside every launch check.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/board-game-kickstarter-launch-day-checklist
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Copy the 30 checks into your project plan.
- [ ] Name one owner for publishing, email, ads and support.
- [ ] Set the launch time and timezone with the whole team.
- [ ] Write down the approved daily ad cap.

## The full checklist by phase

### 90 to 60 days out

- [ ] Reward contents frozen
- [ ] Supplier quotes in hand
- [ ] Funding goal covers delivery
- [ ] Every reward priced with a margin
- [ ] Shipping approach chosen

### 60 to 30 days out

- [ ] Signup route tested
- [ ] Kickstarter draft and target date set
- [ ] Prelaunch page live
- [ ] Referral link for each channel
- [ ] First capped ad test running

### 30 to 8 days out

- [ ] Story and reward graphics approved
- [ ] Video and rules open on a phone
- [ ] Reviewer copies delivered
- [ ] Submitted with time for changes
- [ ] Launch email drafted

### The final week

- [ ] One owner per launch job
- [ ] Launch time and timezone agreed
- [ ] Daily ad cap approved
- [ ] Launch email test delivered
- [ ] Fallback message ready

### Launch day

- [ ] Published by hand
- [ ] Live page checked on a phone
- [ ] Email sent with the live link
- [ ] Ads on after the destination check
- [ ] First review booked

### Days 1 to 3

- [ ] Repeated questions logged
- [ ] Page fixes approved
- [ ] Spend compared with margin
- [ ] First backer update posted
- [ ] Dropped pledges checked

## Planning example

Keep the launch email ready while a second person checks the live campaign on a phone. If the deluxe reward shows the wrong contents, hold the announcement until the creator fixes the offer. Record the decision on the run sheet so ads and support follow the same plan.

## Check your work

The campaign, the announcement and every ad should all lead to the same approved live offer.
