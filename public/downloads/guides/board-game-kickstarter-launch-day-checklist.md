# Write the first-hour run sheet.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/board-game-kickstarter-launch-day-checklist
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Verify each reward price and its contents.
- [ ] Check any later shipping or tax explanation.
- [ ] Open every download and external media link.
- [ ] Confirm the launch time and timezone with the team.

## Planning example

Keep the email ready while a second person checks the public campaign on a phone. If the deluxe reward has the wrong contents, hold the announcement until the creator confirms and corrects the offer. Record the decision in the run sheet so advertising and support follow the same plan.

## Check your work

The campaign, announcement and advertising links should all lead to the approved public offer.
