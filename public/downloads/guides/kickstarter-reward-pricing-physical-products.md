# Build one pricing sheet that runs every reward from landed cost to what you keep.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-reward-pricing-physical-products
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Add up landed cost per unit: factory price, packaging, a share of tooling, freight and duties.
- [ ] Add pick and pack, about 8% plus $0.20 in fees on a US pledge, a reserve and your ad cost per backer.
- [ ] Set the future retail price, then place the Kickstarter price and a capped early bird below it.
- [ ] Price a two pack and check it earns more per backer than a single unit.
- [ ] Raise landed cost by 15% and confirm every tier still leaves money.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your landed cost is $21, pick and pack is $3 and ads cost $8 per backer. A $59 Kickstarter price pays $4.92 in fees and a $4.20 reserve, leaving $17.88. A $49 early bird leaves $8.68, so you cap it rather than leave it open.

## Check your work

Every tier stays positive after a 15% cost rise, and each one shows its saving against the retail price.
