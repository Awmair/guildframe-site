# Set a goal you could deliver on if the campaign ended exactly on target.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-funding-goal
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-26

- [ ] Add the cost of making and shipping every reward at the goal itself.
- [ ] Include the factory minimum order, tooling and any certification.
- [ ] Add platform and payment fees at your country's current rates.
- [ ] Add a buffer for surprises, then round up to a clear number.
- [ ] Check that the goal still delivers if most backers choose the cheapest reward.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: a creator needs $20,000 to cover a minimum order, shipping and fees, so the goal is set at $22,000 with a buffer. A lower goal would look easier to reach but would leave them short if the campaign ended on target.

## Check your work

If the campaign ends exactly at the goal, every reward can still be made and shipped without money you do not have.
