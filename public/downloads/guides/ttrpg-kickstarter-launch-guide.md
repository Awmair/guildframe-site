# Lock your RPG's launch window and delivery route before you design rewards.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/ttrpg-kickstarter-launch-guide
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Choose Zine Quest, Zinetopia or your own date, and note any application deadline.
- [ ] Decide how backers will get their PDFs, and who sends the files and when.
- [ ] Publish a free quickstart, one shot or sample spread that links to your prelaunch page.
- [ ] Write down the system, any books a group needs and what one session looks like.
- [ ] Select the tabletop roleplaying subcategory and check it again before review.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you are launching a short zine and want Zinetopia. That means applying by January 4, 2027, launching and ending between February 2 and 19, and running and fulfilling on BackerKit. If your readers mostly back on Kickstarter instead, your own date with a longer prelaunch may suit the zine better.

## Check your work

A game master who reads only your page and rewards should know what to bring to the first session and when each format arrives.
