# Name the one main reason your campaign fell short and write the fix beside it.

Author: Umair at Guildframe

- [ ] Export your dashboard numbers: page visits, referrers, pledges and dropped pledges.
- [ ] Answer the four diagnosis questions: traffic, conversion, goal and price.
- [ ] Post an honest update to backers with a signup link for the relaunch.
- [ ] Rebuild the goal from what you need, not what you hoped to raise.
- [ ] Set a relaunch date only once your list is clearly bigger than last time.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your page had plenty of visits from your own email list, but few of them pledged and many dropped at checkout. That points to conversion, not traffic. So the relaunch starts with a new opening section, clearer photos and simpler tiers, and the date can come sooner than if you had to grow the list.

## Check your work

You can say in one sentence why the first campaign failed and what will be different on the relaunch page.
