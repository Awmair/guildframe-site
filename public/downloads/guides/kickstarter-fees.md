# Put every Kickstarter fee into your budget sheet before you set the goal.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-fees
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Write down your expected average pledge and backer count.
- [ ] Check your country's processing rate on Kickstarter's fee page.
- [ ] Work out the fees on each reward tier, including PDF and $1 tiers.
- [ ] Add fee lines for late pledges and pledge manager payments.
- [ ] Divide what you need after fees by the share you keep.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: you need $30,000 after fees and expect a $40 average pledge on a US project. Each $40 pledge loses $3.40, so you keep 91.5 cents of every dollar. Dividing $30,000 by 0.915 gives a goal of about $32,800, before you leave room for pledges that fail to collect.

## Check your work

Change one reward price in your sheet and confirm the fees, the payout and the goal all update with it.
