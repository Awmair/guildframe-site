# Answer the beginner questions that apply to you before you plan a campaign.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/kickstarter-for-beginners
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-11-25

- [ ] Check that you are 18 or older and live in a supported country, with ID and a bank account there.
- [ ] Decide whether you launch as an individual or a business, and ask an accountant about tax.
- [ ] Write down your goal, your campaign length and the fees on one typical pledge.
- [ ] Mark when money could arrive: 14 days after the deadline, plus your bank's time.
- [ ] List what you would owe backers if you fund, through to the last box arriving.

## Planning example

Hypothetical example: your 30 day campaign ends on a Friday. Kickstarter spends 14 calendar days collecting and processing pledges, then your bank may take another 3 to 14 business days. So a manufacturer deposit due the week after your deadline needs other money, and that belongs in your plan now.

## Check your work

You should be able to explain to a friend, in your own words, what happens to backers' money if you fund and if you don't.
