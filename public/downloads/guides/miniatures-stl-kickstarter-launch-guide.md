# Keep digital files, physical models and licence terms separate.

Guide: https://guildframe.com/guides/miniatures-stl-kickstarter-launch-guide
Author: Umair at Guildframe
Updated: 2026-10-28

- [ ] Label each reward as physical, digital or mixed.
- [ ] Show scale and what the test prints demonstrate.
- [ ] Explain permitted use and delivery format.

## Planning example

A terrain render can suggest a complete table while the reward contains only a small file pack. List the included pieces and show a scale reference. If supports or commercial permissions are separate, make those distinctions visible before a buyer chooses a reward.

## Check your work

Review the reward without looking at its images: the written contents should still explain exactly what is delivered.
