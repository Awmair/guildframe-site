## Final campaign checks
- [ ] Story, graphics and native rewards use the same names and quantities.

- [ ] Rules, reviews and videos open without private access.
- [ ] Prototype and render labels are beside their images.
- [ ] Reward differences and optional extras are clear on a phone.
- [ ] Shipping collection timing and delivery estimates agree everywhere.
- [ ] All intended Gamefound sections/rewards are published in the correct stage.
- [ ] The public view has been checked outside any test override.
- [ ] No placeholders or unapproved claims remain.
- [ ] The creator has confirmed platform approval and the launch action.
