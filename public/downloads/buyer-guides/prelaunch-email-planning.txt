Required reachable subscribers = desired email backers divided by assumed
subscriber to backer rate. Round the result up to a whole subscriber.

Illustration: a creator wants 200 backers overall and plans for 80 from email.
Assumed rate          Calculation         Required reachable subscribers
4%                    80 / 0.04           2,000
8%                    80 / 0.08           1,000
12%                   80 / 0.12           667, rounded up

These assumptions are invented sensitivity inputs. They do not explain how
to acquire the subscribers or prove that they will pledge. The remaining
120 backers in the example still need a plan. Funding and reward economics
need a separate cost model.

Do not add email and follower counts as if everyone were a different person.
A report showing 1,000 email subscribers and 600 platform followers does not
establish 1,600 unique people. One person may receive both notifications.
A platform follow is not permission to import someone into your email list.

SAMPLE WELCOME EMAIL FOR FICTIONAL HARBOUR COUNCIL
Use only after replacing the details and checking the signup promise.

Subject: Your first decision in Harbour Council

Thanks for asking for a look at Harbour Council. It is a strategy game for
two to four players about running rival harbours. Each turn, you claim cargo
from a shared market to store or use for a shipping contract. Taking the last
timber crate can finish your contract while leaving another harbour short.

Read the two page sample round: [link to the promised sample]

It shows the starting board, both choices and what happens next.

I will send development notes and the Kickstarter launch announcement to
this address. You can reply with a question about the game or leave using
the unsubscribe link below.

[Creator name]
[Studio contact details]
[Working unsubscribe link from your email service]

BEFORE SENDING
- The form promises these messages and the sample actually exists.
- The sender and reply address belong to your studio.
- A test subscriber appears once in the intended list.
- The sample and unsubscribe links work in the received message.
- The message remains understandable on a phone and with images hidden.
- Each product fact has a named approver.
