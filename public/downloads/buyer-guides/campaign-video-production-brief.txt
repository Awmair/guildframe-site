ILLUSTRATIVE SEQUENCE FOR FICTIONAL HARBOUR COUNCIL
1. Identify the game: show the full board and player work areas.
2. Show a decision: use the last timber for a contract or store stone for later.
3. Show the result: the contract is complete; the timber is unavailable to
   the next player. Check that this sequence follows the approved rules.
4. Explain the reward: show core contents separately from deluxe replacements.
5. Explain the funding purpose: the creator describes the next production step.

This is an invented shot plan, not existing footage or a client case study.

ACCEPTANCE
- Every product, reward and funding statement matches the approved facts.
- Prototype material is identified where needed.
- The sample turn shows a decision and consequence accurately.
- The main message is understandable with sound off.
- Speech is clear with sound on; captions are accurate.
- Important text and components survive the actual vertical crop.
- The files play in the intended destination, including embedded videos.
- The campaign page still explains the offer to someone who skips the video.
