WORKED EXAMPLE: FICTIONAL HARBOUR COUNCIL
Inputs: final artwork, draft rules and approved core/deluxe reward matrix.
Requested work: page outline and copy; a sample turn; core box spread;
core/deluxe comparison; optional card pack panel; production/delivery copy;
agreed exports and editable files.
Acceptance: the sample turn follows the rules; every component count matches
the approved sheet; deluxe replacements are clear; text is readable in the
phone preview; final files open and match the approved version.

This example describes a job, not a standard section count or price. If the
creator adds a language or changes rewards after approval, ask for the scope,
fee and date effect in writing before that extra work starts.
