## Keyword research

**138 target phrases** are mapped to all 46 routes. 9 are exact observed autocomplete phrases; the rest are labelled editorial targets inferred from the service, category and platform terminology. Priorities reflect closeness to a campaign design enquiry, not estimated traffic or keyword difficulty.

Google and Bing autocomplete were captured for twelve seeds each. The complete report includes 36 primary-source URLs and explains which observations support the service wording. Relevant first-party provider offers were studied for scope language, not treated as a verified ranking survey.

Google Keyword Planner redirected to sign-in. Search volume, CPC, advertiser competition and forecasts are unavailable. Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools account performance was also unavailable. Empty niche suggestions do not mean zero demand. Unreliable direct search HTML/RSS results were excluded from ranking conclusions.

## Enquiry measurement

The initial page-view event waits for analytics initialization. Exact AI UTM aliases and real host/subdomain boundaries improve attribution. Page views retain only valid campaign UTM parameters; enquiry events omit private form fields.

`generate_lead` fires after a successful Formspree response, not after clicking the button or receiving an error. A free mockup request carries no $975 revenue value. It records the selected category/platform, page and available AI attribution. Email delivery and receipt in the owner's GA4 property remain separate checks.

- **192 static checks passed**, including metadata, sitemap, canonicals, forms, links/anchors, structured data, dates, source links and published-offer consistency.
- ESLint, TypeScript and the production static build passed.
- **92 browser route checks**: all 46 pages at 1440px and 390px; no horizontal overflow or uncaught page errors. Desktop/mobile previews inspected.
- Form tests intercepted requests: failure preserves entries and records no lead; a successful retry records one lead and prevents a duplicate request. No real enquiry was sent.

A working build and valid metadata do not prove indexing or higher rankings. Production verification checks the deployed HTML and assets; owner reports are still needed to measure discovery and enquiries.
