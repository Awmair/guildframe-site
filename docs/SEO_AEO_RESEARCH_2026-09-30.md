# Guildframe SEO, AEO and GEO research

Captured: 2026-09-30. Research only; no account, campaign, billing, outreach or production settings changed.

## Decision

Prioritize the existing campaign service and category pages around **Kickstarter and Gamefound campaign page copy and graphics**, with the **$975 USD** scope and **free opening-section mockup** stated clearly. Improve the usefulness of existing guides and real-project evidence before adding more pages. Public autocomplete supports several relevant terms; authenticated demand and performance metrics remain unavailable.

The current offer was verified on [Guildframe](https://guildframe.com/). Local page targeting was checked against `app/campaign-pages.ts`, `app/campaign-content.ts` and `docs/CAMPAIGN_REBRAND.md`. Recommendations below are editorial judgments, not predictions of search traffic, rankings or funding.

## Official SEO, AEO and GEO requirements

### Google technical eligibility

Public, indexable content must be accessible to Googlebot and return HTTP 200; meeting the requirements does not guarantee indexing. Test actual rendered URLs, not just source files. Use URL Inspection/Page Indexing when account access is available. [Google technical requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/technical)

Google recommends distinct, concise and descriptive titles, with a clear main heading. It does not prescribe a universal title character limit. Descriptions should be unique and specific; snippets may instead come from page text and can differ by query. [Title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link), [snippet guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet)

### Google AI search: current 2026 guidance

Google's current guide treats generative AI search as SEO: prioritize first-hand, useful content, crawl/index quality and a good page experience. It rejects required tiny content chunks, exact query-variation pages and special AI schema. Google Search ignores `llms.txt` for visibility and rankings. Its eligibility guidance also references the Search Console inclusion control. [AI optimization guide, updated 2026-07-10](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)

The **Search generative AI control** is documented as globally rolled out on 2026-08-31. Default inclusion allows links/content in AI Overviews, AI Mode and covered Discover features; exclusions can be inherited from parent properties. Check the actual Guildframe property without assuming its setting. This control is distinct from AI-training choices. [Control documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024)

The **Generative AI performance report** documents AI Overviews/AI Mode impressions by page, country, device and date, including text/multimodal filters. The documentation notes sufficient impressions are needed for visibility of the report. No Guildframe report was inspected; absence in an account must not be reported as zero demand. [Report documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139)

Google's changelog says FAQ rich results stopped appearing **2026-05-07**, with the FAQ feature documentation removed on **2026-06-15**. Visible FAQs can still answer customers' questions. Existing FAQPage data should not be promoted as a Google rich-result opportunity. [Current documentation changelog](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)

### Bing and Microsoft AI search

Bing's current guidelines connect traditional crawling/indexing with Copilot/grounding eligibility. They recommend canonical URLs, accurate sitemap freshness, standard crawlable links, indexable content, focused pages, clear entities and text that can stand alone. Structured data must match visible content. Relevant images should support readable text. `noindex` excludes indexing; `noarchive` prevents Copilot/grounding use, while `nocache` limits grounding depth. Stable URLs are preferable to unnecessary moves. [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a)

Access note: the direct Bing help URL rendered a JavaScript shell; its first-party indexed full text was retrieved through web search. Account settings and Bingbot's live view were not inspected.

Microsoft separately recommends clear headings, direct answers, comparisons and precise contextual claims. Treat this as useful editorial guidance, not a guaranteed citation formula or a Google requirement. Essential scope/price should be readable without opening an accordion. [Microsoft content guidance](https://www.about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/october-2025/optimizing-your-content-for-inclusion-in-ai-search-answers)

Bing's **AI Performance** report measures total citations, average cited pages, sampled grounding queries and URL-level citations across supported Microsoft/partner surfaces. Citations do not indicate answer placement, authority or ranking. Track them separately from website visits and enquiries. [Bing announcement, 2026-02-10](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/)

IndexNow can notify participating engines of added, updated or removed URLs. It is an optional discovery/freshness action after real publishing, not an indexing guarantee; use a valid ownership key and canonical production URLs. [IndexNow documentation](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation)

### OpenAI search access

`OAI-SearchBot` governs automatic ChatGPT search discovery; permit it in robots.txt and verify CDN access for its official IP ranges. `GPTBot` handles potential training crawls independently. `ChatGPT-User` is user-triggered retrieval, not the control for appearing in search; robots.txt may not apply to those user actions. Do not present training permission as necessary for search visibility. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)

### Structured data and visual evidence

Use one consistent Guildframe organization identity, true contact details and the current logo. Organization markup can help disambiguation; it does not require inventing a street address, telephone or registration number. [Google organization guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization)

Represent campaign design as a `Service`, with provider and an `Offer` at `975`/`USD` if it matches the visible offer. This is a semantic recommendation, not a promise of a dedicated Google Service rich result. [Schema.org Service](https://schema.org/Service)

Guides can use Article/BlogPosting with a real author, relevant image and truthful dates; breadcrumbs should match their navigation. Preserve original publication dates and change modification dates only for actual updates. [Article guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article), [breadcrumb guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/breadcrumb)

Markup must describe accessible visible content. Do not turn unrelated reviews or campaign fundraising totals into a Guildframe aggregate rating. Self-serving Organization/LocalBusiness reviews are not eligible for Google's review-star feature. [Structured data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies), [review guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet)

Real campaign examples should identify the project, Guildframe's role and an original platform link, with captions explaining the work. Concept mockups must remain labelled. Describe imagery accurately, provide useful surrounding text and preserve readable mobile images. [Google image guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images)

## Google Ads Keyword Planner: verified limitation and next export

[The public planner page](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/) describes it as a free tool, but its access FAQ says to complete Ads setup/create a campaign and use Expert Mode where necessary. [Current Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243?hl=en) specifically says completed setup including billing information is required for basic suggestions. The public descriptions differ in detail. The app route [Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/aw/keywordplanner/home) redirected to sign-in during this research. No sign-in, account creation, billing entry, campaign creation or spending occurred. Do not assert that spend is required based on this evidence.

When an already configured authorized account is available, export the clusters above through Discover new keywords and historical search metrics. Keep country, date range, language, network and currency with the export. A useful baseline is separate US/UK/Canada/Australia English reports over the last twelve full months, Google search only; this is a proposed research configuration, not a claim about where Guildframe's customers currently live. Report ranges exactly if Google returns ranges; report blanks/dashes as unavailable.

Google defines average monthly searches using the selected period, location/network and close variants; values are rounded. “Competition” describes advertisers, not organic SEO difficulty. Top-of-page bid ranges are historical ad bid information, not measured Guildframe CPC or an SEO forecast. Avoid summing close synonyms into a supposed market size. [Google metric definitions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en)
