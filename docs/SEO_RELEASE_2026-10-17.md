# Tabletop service and campaign evidence update

This release responds to the 17 October owner dashboard audit. GSC listed 18 indexed pages and 51 discovered but not indexed. The existing sitemap, readable static HTML, canonical URLs, metadata and internal reachability already worked. The release strengthens the existing pages and adds evidence rather than creating more pages for keyword variants.

## Published content

- Thirteen campaign service and category pages gain specific delivery steps, required inputs, handoff details and booking answers. Each Service entity now identifies its own canonical service page.
- Twenty four launch guides gain practical planning material. The marketing plan includes an illustrative eight week schedule and launch decisions. The budget guide separates reward economics, acquisition allowance and the full cash budget. Email, agency selection, page review and design scope guides include working examples or comparison sheets. Other launch guides gain topic specific review checklists.
- The thirteen existing post campaign guides gain relevant next steps or a contextual path into launch planning. Their historical publication dates remain intact; layout only additions do not imply a fresh review of every third party product or regulation.
- Three case studies document ScentedRealms, FutureProof Terrain and Quiver Time. They identify the actual creative contribution, preserve creator image credits and link to the original Kickstarter campaigns. Existing client excerpts remain verbatim. No new testimonials, funding claims or advertising results are invented.
- The homepage, navigation, relevant service pages and guide examples link to the case studies. Established Shopify and benchmark traffic pages link contextually to launch help.
- The 2024 benchmark retains its historical year and dataset. A separate October 2026 source review explains the coverage and the limits of using an aggregate success rate or mean as a forecast.
- The sitemap now contains 73 canonical routes. Case studies appear in the public content description and search intent inventory. AI visibility remains subject to ordinary crawl, indexing and citation decisions.

## Keyword decisions

Google Ads research used US, English, Google network and October 2025 through September 2026. Kickstarter advertising appeared in the 100 to 1,000 monthly bucket. Kickstarter marketing agency, board game marketing agency, campaign design and marketing budget appeared in 10 to 100 buckets. Narrower Gamefound terms appeared in 0 to 10 buckets. These are broad historical ranges; close variants overlap and advertiser competition is not organic difficulty.

Existing URLs retain their intent: the homepage and launch service cover agency selection, the advertising service covers paid ads, the design page covers campaign creative, and the existing guides answer planning and hiring questions. The new work pages are evidence about distinct real projects.

## Verification and release

Before release: lint, TypeScript, strict production preflight, 299 static assertions and 146 browser page checks across 73 routes at 375 and 1366 pixels passed. The browser audit used one temporary page, reduced motion and stubbed external analytics. It verifies layout and rendered SEO; it is not a field Core Web Vitals or recipient delivery test.

Production uses the existing Cloudflare Pages Git integration. Verify the deployment and live pages before requesting indexing. Submit the materially changed sitemap once, use the existing IndexNow route, then make individual requests for the priority service pages and case studies. An accepted request is a crawl queue receipt, not confirmation of indexing or rankings.

The existing successful response generate_lead event remains in place with service, category, launch timing and page context. Do not equate impressions with enquiries or invent qualified lead counts. External credit requests and outreach still require a human approved recipient and message; this release sends none.
