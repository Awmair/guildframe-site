FutureProof Terrain is a modular wargaming terrain system from Snot Goblin Gaming. Umair designed the Kickstarter campaign presentation around its practical uses, working with the creator's product imagery.

The campaign graphic shows an assembled building beside separate walls and floors. That relationship matters: a player needs to understand how the pieces become terrain they can use on a table.

Short benefit headings keep the graphic easy to scan. The Snot Goblin identity and green accents frame the product against a dark background, so the terrain still carries the explanation.

For a modular product, the same logic carries through the campaign: show the contents, an assembled view and the differences between rewards. This is campaign creative from the original 2024 launch. It does not measure advertising performance or attribute the campaign's funding.
