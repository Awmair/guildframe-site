Turn your existing artwork into a production brief

Give each asset a job.

List the artwork, component files and gameplay footage you already have. Then decide which assets still need making.

For each proposed render, GIF, ad variant or trailer, write the message, destination, format and deadline. Add the source files, rights, approval owner and review rounds. A script should say what appears on screen as well as what the viewer hears.

If you already have a publisher team and finished artwork, this brief can focus on concepts, messaging, scripts and production. You do not need to start the whole campaign again.
