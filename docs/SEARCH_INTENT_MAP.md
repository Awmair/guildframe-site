Updated October 3, 2026. Guildframe is a tabletop crowdfunding launch studio. Query targets reflect intent and the actual offer; no volume, CPC or ranking prediction is invented.

- Homepage: tabletop Kickstarter agency, broader launch offer, creative proof and direct project enquiry.
- `/kickstarter-launch-services`: scope, owners, proposal and the work involved in a full launch.
- `/kickstarter-prelaunch-marketing`: landing pages, email, audience tests and readiness before funding opens.
- `/kickstarter-advertising`: hiring an ad manager, creative testing, account access, media budget and reporting.
- `/kickstarter-campaign-management`: launch-day checks, updates, support period and approvals.
- `/gamefound-launch-services`: full launch work adapted to Gamefound.
- `/post-campaign-support`: backer communication, pledge-manager content and optional store handoff.
- `/campaign-design`: copy and graphics scope, the single fixed design price, free opening mockup and creative evidence.
- Existing five category pages and Gamefound design: specific product information, artwork, gameplay and reward presentation. They keep their design intent and link to the broader launch services.
- 22 launch guides: practical research and decision support, including marketing plans, email, ad budgets, page readiness, platform preparation and live updates.
- `/buy`: branded enquiry and booking process.
- About, author and editorial pages: the operator, confirmed advertising role, sources and commercial transparency.
- 13 after-funding articles and seven resources: useful retained answers at established URLs, with links to relevant launch help.
