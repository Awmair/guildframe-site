# Search intent and page ownership

Updated 2026-09-30. The campaign design offer supersedes the earlier Shopify offer.

The complete mapping is in [KEYWORD_TARGETS_2026-09-30.csv](KEYWORD_TARGETS_2026-09-30.csv). One intent group has one preferred URL; query variants belong on that page, not on new synonym pages.

- Homepage: introduce tabletop Kickstarter page design, the price and the free opening mockup.
- `/campaign-design`: hiring decision, exact copy/graphics scope, written brief, past work and objections.
- Five category pages: different product information and artwork needs for board games, cards/TCGs, RPGs, miniatures/terrain and accessories.
- `/gamefound-campaign-design`: Gamefound story/reward layout and preview preparation.
- Eight launch guides: costs, page checklist, designer brief, rewards, RPG sample pages, platform differences, image sizes/mobile readability and hiring timing.
- `/buy`: branded enquiry and booking process, not a second generic service landing page.
- Author/About/editorial pages: who provides the service and writes the guidance.
- Thirteen after-funding guides and six references: preserve their practical answers and existing URLs. These are maintenance targets, not the current commercial service.

Priorities reflect proximity to a campaign design enquiry and the site's offer, not measured traffic, CPC or keyword difficulty. No geographic landing pages were created: Guildframe serves English-speaking creators directly and no office or service-area claims were supplied.

Before splitting, merging, redirecting or retiring a page, inspect actual query/page data in Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. Two related pages are not proof of harmful cannibalization; check whether the same intent alternates between URLs and whether enquiries suffer.
