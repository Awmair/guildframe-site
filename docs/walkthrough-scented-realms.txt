ScentedRealms brings fantasy scents to a roleplaying table. For its Kickstarter campaign, Umair developed the visual direction and campaign presentation. The first job was to make an unfamiliar accessory understandable.

The opening composition puts the physical products in the foreground. Visitors can see the ScentEngine, the packaged scents and the wider collection, while the fantasy imagery establishes the setting.

Grouping the products gives the collection a visible scale. The red frame, dark background and fantasy colour keep the presentation consistent, while the products remain the focus.

For a launch like this, the page needs to explain what the accessory is, how it fits into a session and what a backer receives. Umair's contribution was the campaign presentation. The product, identity and supplied imagery belong to the creator.
