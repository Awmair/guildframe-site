## What current SEO/AEO/GEO guidance supports

1. **Useful, original material before more URLs.** Google's current AI optimization guide emphasizes non-commodity content, relevant images and video, clear structure and ordinary search fundamentals. It expressly warns against creating pages for every variation of a query just to influence AI responses. It says there is no special AI schema, mandatory chunking method or ideal word count; Google Search ignores `llms.txt` for ranking and visibility. Keep strong practical examples, signed authorship, real work evidence and sources. [Google AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).
2. **Indexing remains a prerequisite.** A supporting link in Google's AI features must be indexed and eligible for a normal search snippet. Crawl permission, canonical URLs, static readable content and useful internal links still matter; eligibility does not guarantee inclusion. [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).
3. **FAQ rich results have been retired.** Current Google documentation records that FAQ rich results stopped appearing from **7 May 2026**, with FAQ documentation removed in June. The old FAQ documentation URL redirects to the changelog. Do not promise FAQ rich-result eligibility based on the older government/health-only rule. Keep useful visible questions and answers; accurate Article, BreadcrumbList, Organization and author markup remain appropriate. [Google Search documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).
4. **Measure citations separately from visits and leads.** Google's current guide links a Generative AI performance report in Search Console. Bing's AI Performance report measures citations, cited pages and grounding-query activity, not clicks, sales or organic rank. Check the reports if exposed in the owner accounts; retain enquiry conversion tracking for commercial outcomes. [Google AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), [Bing AI Performance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c), [Bing announcement](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/).
5. **Submit actual changes.** Keep canonical routes in the sitemap and notify participating engines of changed URLs through IndexNow. Submission acceptance is not proof of indexing or an AI citation. [Bing announcement](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/).

### 1. Board-game Kickstarter funding-goal calculation

- Suggested slug: `board-game-kickstarter-funding-goal`.
- Main intent: "how to calculate Kickstarter funding goal for a board game"; supporting phrases: "board game Kickstarter minimum funding goal", "Kickstarter manufacturing MOQ funding goal".
- Direct answer: set the goal from the cash required to deliver the promised rewards, including manufacturing commitments, production work, fees, fulfillment obligations and contingency. Check the reward mix and the minimum manufacturing order. A low public target does not reduce those obligations.
- Distinction: the existing advertising-budget article covers acquisition allowance. This article explains whether the funded project itself can be delivered.
- Worked example: a single illustrative $60 reward; $30 variable production/fulfillment costs, assumed 8% platform/payment allowance ($4.80), $6 acquisition cost; $19.20 contribution remains. A $9,600 fixed-cost/contingency requirement needs 500 successfully collected pledges, or $30,000 gross. Replace the assumed 8% with the actual country/platform schedule. Shipping and tax treatment must use a consistent basis. Account separately for inventory bought to meet an MOQ but not pledged for. If contribution is zero or negative, raising the volume does not solve the model.
- Useful table: cost item / quote owner / amount / fixed or per-unit / payment date / confidence. Include a cash-timing check: deposits may fall before platform payout, and failed collections can reduce funds.
- Link to `/guides/kickstarter-advertising-budget-board-games`, `/guides/kickstarter-reward-tier-graphics` and `/kickstarter-launch-services`.
- Primary source: [Kickstarter funding-goal guidance](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236709-how-to-set-the-right-funding-goal-for-your-kickstarter-campaign).

### 2. Kickstarter reward pricing and margin for board games

- Suggested slug: `board-game-kickstarter-reward-pricing`.
- Main intent: "how to price Kickstarter board game rewards"; supporting phrases: "Kickstarter board game reward margin", "deluxe pledge tier pricing board game".
- Direct answer: price each tier against its contents, unit costs, payment fees, acquisition allowance and share of fixed costs. Check that the core game, deluxe tier, bundle and add-ons each remain affordable to fulfill.
- Distinction: reward-tier graphics explains presentation; this explains the economics that must be settled before those graphics are made.
- Worked example: $42 product/fulfillment cost + $6 advertising allowance + $6 fixed-cost allocation + $8 desired contribution after those items = $62 before an illustrative 8% percentage fee. $62 / 0.92 = $67.39. A $69 price leaves $9.48 after the stated $54 costs and assumed percentage fee. Currency is USD; every input is hypothetical. Do not apply the platform fee twice if already included elsewhere.
- Useful table: tier / exact contents / cost at quoted MOQ / weight and shipping effect / price / contribution / stock allocation. Explain why an extra miniature or bigger box can change fulfillment, not just manufacturing.
- Keep MSRP and retail/distributor economics separate. Avoid claiming a standard wholesale margin or mandatory crowdfunding discount. A discount must use a genuine comparison price.
- Link to `/guides/kickstarter-reward-tier-graphics`, the funding-goal guide, `/guides/kickstarter-stretch-goals-planning` and `/campaign-design`.
- Primary sources: [Kickstarter rewards](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236688-how-do-i-add-rewards-to-my-project), [Gamefound reward configuration](https://help.gamefound.com/article/127-creating-rewards-in-crowdfunding), [Kickstarter funding goal](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236709-how-to-set-the-right-funding-goal-for-your-kickstarter-campaign).

### 3. Track Kickstarter campaigns with referral tags and UTMs

- Suggested slug: `kickstarter-referral-tags-utm-tracking`.
- Main intent: "track Kickstarter ads with referral links"; supporting phrases: "Kickstarter UTM tracking", "Kickstarter custom referral tags before launch", "Kickstarter ROAS dropped pledges".
- Direct answer: create named referral links in Kickstarter and use consistent UTMs where Analytics is configured. Treat website visits, email signups, attributed pledges and successfully collected revenue as different measurements.
- Current platform fact: custom referral tags can be created **before launch** in the Promotion tab. The October 22 help article says reports include pledges, attributed amount, share of funding and dropped pledges after collection. It does not provide backer-specific referral data for dropped pledges. Do not repeat old claims that referral tags are available only after launch.
- Practical worksheet: channel / owner / destination / generated Kickstarter referral URL / utm_source / utm_medium / utm_campaign / utm_content / test date. Keep the actual generated referral parameter; do not invent a valid Kickstarter referral ID in the example.
- UTM example for an owned landing page: `?utm_source=creator_newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=orbit_launch&utm_content=rules_preview`. Values are case-sensitive; use a documented naming convention.
- Worked report: $600 ad spend and $1,800 attributed pledges produce 3.0 pledge-value ROAS. If $150 drops, collected attributed revenue is $1,650 and the corresponding ratio is 2.75. Neither ratio is profit. Keep dates and attribution rules alongside the numbers, and do not add conflicting platform totals as if they were independent sales.
- Link to the existing advertising-budget, prelaunch-email and marketing-plan guides, plus `/kickstarter-advertising`.
- Primary sources: [Kickstarter referral tags](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236548-how-do-i-create-a-custom-referral-tag-and-track-referral-stats), [Google Analytics UTM guidance](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952).

### 5. Kickstarter launch-day checklist for a tabletop game

- Suggested slug: `board-game-kickstarter-launch-day-checklist`.
- Main intent: "Kickstarter launch day checklist board game"; supporting phrases: "test Kickstarter campaign before launch", "Gamefound test checkout before crowdfunding".
- Direct answer: finish the page and reward review, confirm platform approval and account readiness, assign the launch button to one person, then verify the public page and every outbound announcement before increasing promotion.
- Current platform facts: Kickstarter launches are manual. Setting a target date does not schedule publication. Project review can take up to three business days and additional revisions can add another review period or longer; allow buffer rather than publishing a guaranteed approval time.
- Useful run sheet: time / task / owner / evidence / stop condition. Separate previous-day checks, publication, first-hour checks and first-day review. Include mobile page, reward contents/prices, regional shipping wording, live URL, email links, referral tags, approved ad caps and support ownership.
- Gamefound distinction: Tester Mode lets invited testers inspect phases and place test orders; it does not send a payment to the operator. A successful test order does not prove a real payment/payout. Include options, product quantities, country/region handling and test exports, then exit Tester Mode.
- Link to `/guides/kickstarter-review-launch-timeline`, `/guides/board-game-kickstarter-page-checklist`, `/guides/gamefound-launch-page-checklist` and `/kickstarter-launch-services`.

### 6. Choose a pledge manager for a board-game Kickstarter

- Suggested slug: `board-game-kickstarter-pledge-manager-checklist`.
- Main intent: "choose pledge manager for board game Kickstarter"; supporting phrases: "Kickstarter pledge manager shipping upgrades checklist", "pledge manager quote board game campaign".
- Direct answer: compare eligible systems against the actual order tasks: surveys, reward changes, add-ons, shipping and tax handling, late buyers, exports, support and total fees. Test representative orders and data exports before committing.
- Distinction: the existing BackerKit/Shopify/Gamefound guide describes platform roles. This is a purchase/selection checklist with a requirements matrix and handoff test.
- Include Kickstarter's **native Pledge Manager** as an available route for eligible projects; do not imply every creator needs a third-party vendor. Its help says there is no upfront cost, with fees on payments made inside the tool. Verify current eligibility and contract terms.
- Operational fact: Kickstarter Late Pledges must end before its Pledge Manager launches; the manager can be configured to accept new backers. Gamefound similarly distinguishes Late Pledge from the shipping-collection stage. These are not identical to permanent Shopify retail orders.
- Quote matrix: setup fee / charge on already raised funds / charge on new money / processing / refunds / software term / support / export / tax responsibilities. Avoid claiming all services use the same fee base.
- Test matrix: base game, deluxe edition, language choice, add-on, upgrade, late buyer, changed country, missing response, mixed digital/physical reward and a fulfillment export. Record reward/item identifiers, quantities, paid/unpaid state and address status.
- Link to `/guides/backerkit-vs-shopify-vs-gamefound`, `/guides/kickstarter-late-pledges-vs-shopify`, `/guides/what-happens-after-kickstarter-is-funded` and `/kickstarter-launch-services`.

## Current platform details worth preserving in launch guides

- **Kickstarter prelaunch:** first-time creators need project review/approval to access the public prelaunch setup; repeat creators may access it after the stated preparation steps. Prelaunch updates notify followers. The app does not display the editable prelaunch description. Do not merge a follower count with an email subscriber count as if everyone were unique. [Prelaunch setup](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236379-setting-up-your-project-s-pre-launch-page).

Fees are5% plus roughly3–5% processing under the current help description. Its recommended10% late-price increase is a recommendation, not a mandatory rule or a universal margin solution. [Late Pledge setup](https://help.kickstarter.com/hc/en-us/articles/24875583879067-How-do-I-add-Late-Pledges-to-my-project).

## Guide quality and release acceptance

For every guide: keep a direct opening answer, descriptive headings, a practical example or decision aid, source links beside material platform claims, an honest author/review date, contextual internal links and a useful service next step. Retain original publication dates. Do not refresh dates solely for a shared layout or decorative asset change.

For visuals: use an actual explanatory diagram or infographic tied to the article's decision, with a text equivalent. Preserve readable labels, responsive sizing, explicit dimensions and meaningful alt text; avoid baking the only copy of important instructions into an image. The user has deferred newly generated graphics until the built-in generator works; do not substitute a different generation provider without their authorization.

Acceptance should include rendered static content; one descriptive H1; unique sensible title/description; canonical and sitemap consistency; reachable internal links; accurate Article/Breadcrumb/author data; no accidental noindex; crawlable image assets; image dimensions; mobile overflow and readable tables. Record real user performance separately from synthetic layout checks. Submit changed/new URLs only after deployment is verified, and report indexing requests as requests rather than completed indexing.
