## Recommendation

Add **ten distinct launch guides**, answering practical decisions before a tabletop campaign goes live. Expand the existing image-size and reward-graphics guides instead of creating competing URLs. Priorities below reflect relevance to Guildframe's customers and useful platform differences; they are not search-volume estimates.

Keep the service offer consistent: **Kickstarter and Gamefound campaign page copy and graphics, $975 USD, using supplied game artwork, with a free opening-section mockup**. Video production, new illustration, rules development, advertising, manufacturing and fulfilment are separate services. Do not promise a funding result, delivery date or fixed revision count. Preserve useful legacy URLs while the separately collected owner performance data is assessed.

The ten new topics are prelaunch versus preview pages; gameplay and rulebooks; campaign video planning; stretch goals; shipping and delivery presentation; prototype and risk disclosure; platform review and launch timing; Gamefound stage visibility and launch checks; card-game/TCG contents and playability; and physical-miniature/STL reward readiness.

## Verified platform facts for authors

These are compact source summaries. Keep platform requirements, platform recommendations and Guildframe's editorial advice visibly distinct. Most Kickstarter Help Center articles below display August 6, 2026; the media article says “Updated this week.” The Gamefound pages display July 22, 2026, except Getting started, August 20, 2026. Do not invent a precise update date for a relative date label.

### Kickstarter

**K1 — Prelaunch and preview.** Desktop prelaunch includes title, subtitle, image and editable description; the Kickstarter app omits that added description. First-time creators gain Promotion access after review approval; repeat creators can access it earlier. Kickstarter recommends sharing prelaunch at least one week before intended launch. Preview and prelaunch have different URLs; both support launch notifications. Followers cannot be directly messaged, but prelaunch updates notify them by email. [Prelaunch page documentation](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236379-setting-up-your-project-s-pre-launch-page)

**K2 — Story contents.** Kickstarter asks creators to explain what they are making, how, current progress, budget and their story. Its handbook also recommends a clear schedule and appropriate prototypes or samples. Gameplay diagrams, rules links and component comparisons are Guildframe's application of this guidance, not a mandatory platform page template. [Project information](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236738-what-information-should-i-share-on-my-project-page), [Creator Handbook: Your story](https://www.kickstarter.com/help/handbook/your_story)

**K3 — Story navigation.** Story headings create an interactive table of contents. Images can become section headings when the creator supplies a heading label. Section links work on desktop and mobile web; this feature is not supported in the Kickstarter app. [Table of contents](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236730-how-do-i-add-a-table-of-contents-to-my-project-s-story)

**K4 — Images and embedded media.** Story images must be at most 50 MB; recommended formats are JPEG, PNG and GIF, scaled to at least 700 px before upload. The article does not specify that 700 px is a mandatory width. Uploads are compressed; TIFF, BMP, HEIC and HEIF are unsupported. Some GIF animations become static; animated WebP is the suggested fallback. Story video uses an embedded URL. Project-video uploads allow 5 GB, update-video uploads 3 GB; the project display version is 640 × 360, 16:9. Alt text can be added to draft/live story images; story editing stops after campaign end. [Media documentation](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236740-how-do-i-include-images-or-other-media-in-my-project-description-and-updates)

**K5 — Main and Discovery Mode videos.** Both are optional. The Basics tab accepts a main project video up to 5120 MB. Discovery Mode is a separate app-feed video with a 9:16 vertical aspect ratio and the same maximum file size. Kickstarter recommends 60–90 seconds, clear audio, captions and keeping important material away from edges **for Discovery Mode**. Do not turn that recommendation into a main-video duration requirement. [Video requirements and recommendations](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236743-is-a-project-video-required-to-launch)

**K6 — Reward controls and images.** Tiers specify items, price, delivery and shipping. Optional reward/add-on images require a 3:2 ratio, minimum 348 × 232 px, maximum 50 MB, JPG/PNG/GIF. Tiers and add-ons sort by price; one tier may be featured. A digital main tier cannot accept shippable add-ons. Shippable add-ons depend on the main tier's destination. Local pickup requires a separate tier/add-on. [Adding rewards](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236688-how-do-i-add-rewards-to-my-project)

**K7 — Stretch goals.** Kickstarter says stretch goals are creator-defined, beyond the main target, rather than an official platform feature. Meeting the main goal determines funding regardless of unmet stretch goals. Extra scope can increase production and shipping complexity; the platform recommends careful planning and clear communication, and identifies add-ons as an alternative. [Stretch goals](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236765-what-are-stretch-goals)

**K8 — Shipping presentation.** Preferences distinguishes shipping collected at pledge from collection later through a pledge manager. Shipping collected at pledge counts toward the funding goal. If using a third-party service afterward, Kickstarter recommends a story section explaining timing, collection method and an estimate where possible. [Shipping collection options](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236769-what-options-do-i-have-for-charging-shipping-costs)

**K9 — Delivery dates.** Each tier needs an estimated delivery date. For a bundle, this should reflect when all its contents are expected to arrive. Kickstarter expects updates explaining delays. Its shipping guidance recommends planning weight, dimensions, combined add-ons and buffers early. [Estimated delivery](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236744-what-does-estimated-delivery-date-mean), [Planning reward shipping](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236521-how-do-i-charge-shipping-on-my-rewards)

**K10 — Review and manual launch.** A queued review can take up to three business days; requested changes can add another three business days or longer. High submission volume can extend this, so the platform recommends a buffer. Target Launch Date is a planning aid, not an automatic launch setting. The approved project launches only after the creator's manual launch action. [Review](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236415-my-project-is-awaiting-review-how-does-this-work), [Launching an approved project](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236427-how-do-i-launch-my-project-after-it-s-been-approved)

**K11 — Campaign duration.** Kickstarter allows 1–60 days and recommends 30 days or fewer. The recommendation is not a guarantee of success. Deadline and funding duration cannot be adjusted after launch. [Duration documentation](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236742-what-is-the-maximum-project-duration)

**K12 — Prototype restrictions have a category scope.** The working-prototype requirement and prohibition on photorealistic renders in this source apply to **hardware and product design**. Do not rewrite them as a blanket ban for all Games campaigns or infer that every tabletop accessory is exempt. Confirm the actual category and applicable rules. [Hardware and product design rules](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236428-what-are-the-rules-for-hardware-and-product-design-projects)

**K13 — Board-game rules readiness.** Kickstarter's May 14, 2026 editorial article recommends unguided/blind playtesting to identify what the rulebook communicates without a designer teaching it. This supports a guide about presenting tested gameplay and clearly labelled draft rules; it does not establish a compulsory downloadable rulebook requirement. [How to playtest before launch](https://updates.kickstarter.com/how-to-playtest-your-board-game-before-you-launch-on-kickstarter/)

**K14 — TCG production example.** Kickstarter's March 17, 2026 WeirdCo case study describes a limited test print run used for feedback, an alpha demonstration and production-workflow testing. It is an example from one publisher, not a universal TCG launch requirement or a performance forecast for Guildframe clients. [Cyberpunk TCG case study](https://updates.kickstarter.com/case-study-cyberpunk-tcg-uses-kickstarter-to-solve-the-industrys-biggest-problem/)

### Gamefound

**G1 — Stage sections and media.** Description sections start unpublished and appear only after both publication and the relevant stage's launch. Default crowdfunding sections include story, rewards, risks/challenges and refunds/cancellation. Section names form navigation. Uploaded images convert to WebP; current documentation recommends widths of 840–1200 px, with maximum height 12,000 px and file size 20 MB. Description MP4 uploads have a 20 MB limit and recommended minimum width 840 px; YouTube embedding is available. Its mobile video proportion advice states a maximum ratio of 1:3; treat this as a recommendation, not a universal upload requirement. [Project detailed description](https://help.gamefound.com/article/214-project-detailed-description)

**G2 — Copying and stage locks.** Copying section descriptions works toward future stages, overwrites existing target content and leaves copied sections unpublished. The project URL is locked after preview publication. Further fields lock as stages advance, including the preview description after crowdfunding starts and the campaign description before the successful campaign's pledge manager is published. [Copying descriptions](https://help.gamefound.com/article/281-copying-section-descriptions), [Editing at each stage](https://help.gamefound.com/article/247-editing-guide)

**G3 — Rewards.** Rewards need at least one item and begin unpublished; their items must be published before the reward. The displayed delivery range shows only the starting month/year, with extra delivery remarks in a tooltip. Featured rewards receive list priority. Reward shipping settings are separate from reward-item shipping settings. [Creating rewards](https://help.gamefound.com/article/127-creating-rewards-in-crowdfunding)

**G4 — Native stretch goals.** Gamefound supports manual goals and automatic goals based on backer count, funds or campaign day. Goals use a name, image and description; manual goals require a creator unlock action. Time-based goals are published when crowdfunding begins even when their unlock day is later. [Stretch goal settings](https://help.gamefound.com/article/113-stretch-goals)

**G5 — Timeline and launch preparation.** The Getting started guide recommends story, gameplay/rules, rewards, shipping/tax information, testing and review. Upcoming is the public audience-building stage. Normal Late Pledge does not collect shipping details/fees; Pledge Manager does. Express crowdfunding uses a different collection flow. Do not describe every Gamefound project type as identical. [Getting started](https://help.gamefound.com/article/285-getting-started-with-crowdfunding)

**G6 — Tester Mode.** Authorized testers can preview different phases, user groups and unpublished content. Test checkout saves the order in the system without sending it to the payment operator. A visible blue strip marks test mode; the guide says to exit it afterward. This research performed no invitations, test orders or account changes. [Tester Mode](https://help.gamefound.com/article/366-tester-mode)

**G7 — Product options.** Gamefound supports size, language and colour options on product pieces, not product sets. An option/value cannot be removed after a backer adds it to a pledge; disabling availability has separate behavior. Confirm options before showing them in graphics. This article displays July 24, 2026. [Reward and add-on options](https://help.gamefound.com/article/87-reward-options)
