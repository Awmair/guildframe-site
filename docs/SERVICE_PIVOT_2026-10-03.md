# Tabletop launch service pivot

The owner approved the full website pivot and confirmed that Umair is an experienced paid advertising operator and Guildframe can offer that service. The new offer includes planning, prelaunch, campaign creative, ads and campaign management, with defined post-campaign communication. Broader service fees are quoted. The design-only fee remains in one visible pricing section.

Six new service URLs and four new marketing guides bring the sitemap to 67 canonical pages. Existing design categories and after-funding content retain their original routes. The homepage uses a compact dark hero, generated multi-genre mockups, creative proof, concrete service stages, three engagement scopes, operator information and direct answers to common objections.

New marketing query targets are editorial intent hypotheses, with no invented volume, CPC or difficulty. Previous observed autocomplete and owner Search Console/Bing baselines remain dated. The new content addresses launch planning, email setup, ad economics and live campaign communication.

Guides distinguish platform facts from Guildframe recommendations. Example budgets are explicitly illustrative arithmetic; there is no promised cost per lead, conversion rate, ROAS or profit. Country-specific platform processing charges are not generalized.

All public copy was reviewed with Humanizer. Real testimonials remain verbatim. No fictitious funding totals, paid media results, partnerships, team members or testimonials were added. New hero and social images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Existing concept images provide party, retro comic, minimal abstract, strategy, RPG, miniature and accessory directions.

The contact form captures service interest and launch timing. Existing production form handling remains in place while native Cloudflare sending awaits verified activation; mocked browser success does not establish inbox delivery. Email and message fields stay out of analytics. Root iCloud mail routing is preserved.

Validation: lint, TypeScript and 271 static assertions pass. Browser and production deployment verification are recorded with the delivery artifact, separately from static tests. Updated sitemap submissions require owner-dashboard acceptance; acceptance is not indexing.
