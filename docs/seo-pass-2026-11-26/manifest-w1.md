## Facts used, all verified today on the primary source
Kickstarter help centre: 25 supported countries, 18+, ID, residence, local bank and card rules; what can be funded (something new, no existing product as the goal, no monetary returns, no prohibited items, no rewards the creator did not make); hardware rules (working prototype, no photorealistic renders, manufacturing plan, prior experience); fees 5% plus 3% and $0.20 (micropledge 5% plus $0.05, no fees if unfunded); review up to 3 business days plus 3 per round of changes, high volume can stretch it, algorithm auto approval, all Design & Technology manually reviewed; every editor tab must be complete before review; launch is manual and can't be scheduled; first time creators get the prelaunch page only after submission and approval, repeat creators before review; appeal with 500 characters, declined appeal ends it; 1 to 60 days, 30 or fewer recommended, deadline locked; editable vs locked after launch; shipping at pledge or later, shipping counts toward the goal; pledge manager no upfront cost, usual fees, available only after launch; 14 day collection, 3 to 14 business days bank time, about 28 days; relaunch needs a new draft, URL and title, pledges don't transfer; 15 categories. Kickstarter blog: 12 things (Feb 13 2025: video under two minutes, verify account first, click Continue two business days before launch); concept to fulfillment (Sep 9 2026: 50 days to launch, about 115 days in all, 45 day live stretch); fulfillment timeline (Jan 29 2026: about a year, 18 months for complex products, phase durations). Indiegogo help (updated Oct 13 2026): fixed funding only, 5% plus 3% and $0.20 per transaction, platform upgrade Oct 16 2025; acquired by Gamefound July 22 2025. Gamefound help (updated Aug 10 2026): 5% of the collected amount, nothing charged if the goal is missed.
