## Voice
Same as yesterday's brief: answer first in two sentences, "we" for how Guildframe works (Umair and his team), "you" for what the creator brings, never a run of bare commands, framing sentence and takeaway per section, lead in before every list, sentences 8 to 25 words, specific nouns, no hype words. Hard rules the tests enforce: no em dash, no en dash, no visible hyphenated compound words in copy (write "first time", "fixed price", "post campaign", "add ons" etc.), meta description 90 to 160 characters after HTML encoding (an apostrophe counts as 6 characters, so prefer none), seoTitle under 60 characters (the site appends " | Guildframe").
