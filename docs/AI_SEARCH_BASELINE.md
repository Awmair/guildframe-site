# SEO and AI visibility baseline

Captured 2026-09-30 before this pass. Source commit: `1453b9bb6dddfc55265e8bee642b787ae938a144`.

The site had 46 canonical public routes: seven campaign service/category pages, eight campaign launch guides, thirteen after-funding guides, six references, and the homepage/booking/store-planning/index/trust pages. Unique metadata, a sitemap, server-rendered content, structured data and source links already existed.

The main weakness was the buying message. The homepage said “Campaign design for your next game launch,” several headings described a launch without identifying page copy or graphics, related launch-guide links always selected the first three articles, author markup referred to an unnamed editorial organization, and the successful-enquiry analytics event was absent.

This baseline contains no verified impressions, traffic, ranking positions, indexing coverage, AI citation counts or enquiry totals. Public responses and a successful build do not establish those account metrics.
