# Guildframe campaign identity

Reviewed: 2026-09-30. Selected direction: 03, Playful Precision.

## Offer and audience

Direct enquiries for Kickstarter and Gamefound campaign page design. $975 USD per agreed campaign. A free opening section mockup explores the direction before booking. Contact: umair@guildframe.com. No guaranteed turnaround or funding result. The former Shopify build, Care Plan and paid Build Guide offers are retired from public promotions. Their existing editorial URLs remain as after-funding references.

Categories: board games (strategy, family, party, solo and cooperative), card games and TCGs, tabletop RPGs (books, settings, adventures and zines), miniatures and terrain (physical and digital), dice and accessories.

## Visual system

Cream #FFF5E7, coral #EF644D, petrol #153E40, mint #B7E1C5. Locally hosted Manrope under its SIL Open Font License. A generated interlocking frame mark and rounded wordmark. Transparent panels use blur, saturation, bright edges and soft shadows. Reading surfaces remain opaque. Reduced transparency and contrast preferences have explicit fallbacks.

Motion: one-time scroll reveals (opacity and 24px translation, 600ms), brief card image and card movement on fine-pointer hover, button press feedback. No animation library. Static content is visible without JavaScript; reduced-motion users receive visible stationary content.

## Generated artwork provenance

All new game graphics and the logo were made with the built-in image_gen tool, following the ImageGen skill. WebP conversion, resizing, transparent trimming and favicon extraction use Sharp for delivery optimisation. No downloaded stock artwork or hand-drawn SVG stand-ins were used for these new graphics.

Each scene is original concept imagery, explicitly labelled on the site. It is not client work, a published game, a testimonial or proof of fundraising results. Real campaign assets come from the client.

Common generation brief: finished website asset for Playful Precision; warm cream, coral, petrol and mint palette; high-end art-directed photography; each game has a different original art style; no real brands, licensed characters, watermark, fabricated campaign metrics or customer proof.

Logo prompt used for the successful generation:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create the final isolated Guildframe logo lockup for the Playful Precision brand, with a genuinely transparent background. Exact text: Guildframe. A large crisp bold rounded contemporary geometric sans wordmark in dark petrol #153E40. To its left, a coral #EF644D mark made from two interlocking rounded square frame tiles whose negative space forms a clear compact G. Flat solid colors, no shadows, gradients, texture, scene, slogan, swatches or presentation board. Strong scalable silhouette, balanced kerning, generous transparent margin. Wide horizontal composition approximately 4:1. This is the actual usable logo asset.

Favicons are derived from the generated mark. The social image is an encoded crop of the generated mixed-game scene. Editable brand values and website layouts remain in source code.

## Form handling

The proven Formspree route https://formspree.io/f/mrewkezq is reused. Name, email, game category, platform, optional asset link, brief, subject and source are submitted with a honeypot. Success and error states are accessible. All contact links point to umair@guildframe.com. The inbox receiving endpoint notifications is an external Formspree setting and must be verified there; adding a hidden email field would not configure it.
