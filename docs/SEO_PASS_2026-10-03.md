# Guildframe design and search audit

Reviewed October 4, 2026. Guildframe offers Kickstarter and Gamefound launch planning, prelaunch pages and email, campaign creative, paid advertising, live support and post-campaign communication for tabletop creators. There are 67 canonical pages. The design-only fee appears once, on `/campaign-design#pricing`; broader engagements use scoped quotes.

## Website and reading experience

The homepage keeps the complete introduction and primary action visible at 320×568, 375×667 and 1366×768. Cream, petrol, coral and mint remain the shared identity. The compact hero uses a rotating gallery of six ImageGen tabletop concepts, with pause/play, keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support. The homepage presents real campaign proof followed by a connected five-stage phone conversation timeline. Large HTML project previews follow one fictional card game through planning, audience preparation, campaign creation, live management and handoff. Launch service pages use the corresponding scene. Three short engagement choices replace long homepage service rows; the homepage has five FAQs and one project form.

All 13 service/category pages explain the scope, deliverables, working agreement and common questions. Guides and resources use searchable indexes, with every entry present in server-rendered HTML and available without JavaScript. Articles provide a direct answer, visible sources, author context and a contents menu. Company, author, booking, retained store-planning and 404 pages share the same typography, controls and spacing.

Native smooth scrolling respects reduced motion. Scroll entrances use opacity and a small transform, run once and leave content visible if JavaScript is unavailable. They reinitialize after internal navigation. Mobile navigation supports Escape, outside clicks and keyboard operation; native details remain usable without scripts. Article contents links identify the current section. Reduced transparency and increased contrast have explicit styles. Form inputs use 16px type on phones.

Existing generated artwork is preserved. Campaign and client images have 480px and 960px WebP variants. The transparent logo is a lossless WebP derived from the existing artwork. The original source assets remain in the repository. Display dimensions are reserved. Manrope uses locally hosted Latin and extended Latin variable WOFF2 files; the 24KB Latin font is preloaded instead of the previous 161KB TTF. No new campaign results, testimonials, partners or staff were invented.
