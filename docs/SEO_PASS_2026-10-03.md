# Guildframe website and search audit

Reviewed October 3, 2026. Current offer: Kickstarter and Gamefound campaign page copy and graphics for $975 USD. The free mockup covers one opening campaign section.

## Website changes

All 57 canonical pages use the Playful Precision identity: Manrope, cream, coral, petrol and mint, rounded cards, glass navigation and readable mobile layouts. The obsolete editorial stylesheet was removed. Guides, resources, company pages, retained Shopify pages, booking and the 404 page share the current system. Mobile articles use a collapsible contents menu; headings scroll below the fixed navigation. Existing ImageGen assets and verified client imagery remain in use.

Ten new launch guides cover prelaunch pages, gameplay/rulebooks, campaign video, stretch goals, shipping/delivery, prototypes/risks, review-to-launch timing, Gamefound stage checks, card games/TCGs and miniatures/STL files. Four existing guides received substantive updates to image specifications, reward controls, final page checks and platform differences. New and updated copy went through Humanizer. The launch hub contains 18 guides; 13 after-funding guides retain their useful URLs.

The new campaign worksheet is downloadable as Markdown. The resources hub now leads with launch planning. The internal-link audit found and fixed two orphan pages: booking and TTRPG store planning.

## Keyword and content method

The research file records 40 current Bing/Google autocomplete captures and primary Kickstarter/Gamefound documentation. Suggestions establish wording, not demand volume. The keyword map distinguishes observed suggestions from editorial query hypotheses. Each new page answers a different creator task; spelling variants and nearly identical questions do not get separate URLs.

The article opening gives a direct answer, followed by usable checklists, examples or comparison tables. Platform facts have visible sources. Product examples, placeholder fields and Guildframe's recommendations are labelled. No carrier prices, production readiness, industry-average design prices, funding guarantees or licences were invented.

## Follow-up measurement

After recrawl, compare impressions and clicks for the existing and new URLs separately. Track the launch-guide clusters alongside successful free-mockup enquiries. Keep Google AI impressions, Bing citations and actual referral visits as separate measures. Do not add sampled page rows to a property total or infer business impact from citation share.

For quality backlinks, prepare an approved case study for each verified client project and offer its factual design process, with client permission, to the client's existing project/site editor. Another useful link target is the launch worksheet for tabletop creator communities whose resource guidelines accept original practical material. Links should come from relevant editorial inclusion; no outreach has been sent or link acquisition claimed.
