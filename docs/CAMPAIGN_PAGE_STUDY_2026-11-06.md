## Research question and limits

What specific product explanation, reward differences, shipping terms and public rules or sample offers can a creator inspect in a varied set of public tabletop campaign sources?

The review describes information available in the retrieved sources. It does not test conversion, funding, product quality, fulfilment, campaign outcomes, advertising effectiveness or causation. There is no ranking, success prediction, correlation analysis or statistical population estimate.

This is a convenience sample deliberately selected for product variety and public source availability. Discovery searches included the features being recorded, including how to play, rulebook, quickstart and shipping. That introduces selection and observability bias. Raw feature counts are labelled coverage within this sample and cannot estimate feature prevalence across crowdfunding.

The 25 fixed campaign URLs are the reproducible sampling frame. Another reviewer can retrieve those same sources and repeat the coding; changing search rankings are not treated as a stable sampling mechanism. Campaigns span different launch years, stages, scales and formats. A cancelled campaign remains in the sample.

Read only web tools were used. No signed in browser, personal Chrome profile, mobile renderer or campaign account was used. The web reader can return cached or indexed text, so a retrieval date does not guarantee that every passage is the latest page version. No image content, video playback, PDF content or linked destination permissions were tested. Labels and actual link/embed presence were observable in the returned text.

## Coding dictionary

Each field is `observed`, `unknown` or `not_applicable`.

- **Observed:** A concrete statement, link or labelled embed establishes the feature in reviewed material. This records presence, without validating the creator's factual or commercial claims.
- **Unknown:** Source coverage did not establish the feature. This includes image only sections, navigation only responses, timed out requests and sources not inspected. This does not mean absent.
- **Not applicable:** The field does not fit the declared product scope. The reason remains in the note.

Field rules:

1. **Gameplay explanation:** At least a concrete goal, player action, rule or required game system is stated. The 15 board, card and RPG entries are eligible. The 10 miniature, terrain and accessory entries use not applicable consistently, even where a mixed miniature bundle includes RPG material. Their product use, compatibility or format information remains in their notes.
2. **Reward comparison:** At least two choices or editions differ explicitly in contents, format, quantity or access. A table is not required. Native listings, described bundle differences and explicit edition differences can qualify. A product catalogue with no demonstrated tier distinction does not.
3. **Shipping information:** Text states fee collection, destinations, costs or delivery arrangements. Platform estimated dispatch dates alone do not qualify. Digital only file delivery is described but marked not applicable to physical shipping for Sisters of the Old Blood.
4. **Rules or sample offer:** A public rulebook, quickstart, preview, labelled sample page or test file offer is directly identified. This records link/embed presence and description only. It does not confirm destination access, readability or file validity. A rulebook included in a paid reward alone does not qualify. Ordinary dice/tool accessories use not applicable; miniatures and terrain remain eligible for test file offers.
5. **Mobile legibility:** Unknown for all 25 records. No rendered mobile views were inspected.

A section heading does not prove its contents. An image returned without usable text does not support a conclusion about its layout, wording, quality or mobile legibility. A positive shipping code does not validate an estimate, tax statement or delivery promise.

There are 25 records plus a header. Values use UTF-8 CSV with quoted cells. ## Practical findings and editorial use

- Quartz and Riposte! / Cell Block 18 provide concrete player actions that can serve as examples when briefing a gameplay section.
- POND and MiniMover show different levels of comparison complexity: edition components versus a simple unit quantity choice.
- The Fifth Season and Riposte! / Cell Block 18 state when shipping is collected separately from the pledge decision.
- The Fifth Season and Sophont label public introductory material and explain its scope or revision state.
- Fighting Fantasy Heroes separates digital files, physical miniatures and collector box contents.

These are examples of observable communication choices. Their presence is not evidence of a funding effect.
